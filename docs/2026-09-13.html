Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Sun 13 Sep 2026 · Daysail

Daysail 10:00–18:00

A marginal, changeable day — a decent E breeze early, but rain, fog and gusty NW behind a front make it a keep-it-short call.

The day

This one's a judgment day rather than a slam-dunk. There's a genuinely sailable breeze around — roughly 9–12 knots and the live anemometers are actually reading a touch stronger than the forecast — so the wind itself would make for a pleasant sail. The catch is the weather riding along with it: a wet, foggy setup with rain showers likely and the wind swinging all the way from the east around to the northwest as a front slides through, with squally gusts into the high-20s and low-30s at times. If you go, treat it as a short, keep-an-eye-on-the-sky outing rather than a lazy all-day drift.

Wind

You've got real breeze to work with. Right now it's blowing from the east around 9–12 knots (Conimicut up-bay is gusting near 14), and over the day it'll gradually veer around toward the northwest as the front passes — nice sailing strength through the middle, but expect puffy, gusty moments tied to the showers, so be ready to ease and don't over-canvas. The steadier, more comfortable air lives out toward Hog Island and the East Passage; up inside the harbor and tucked under the Poppasquash shore to the west you'll find the usual soft, patchy spots, especially once the wind works into the northwest and that shore blocks it. Best window is mid-morning to early afternoon before the wind gets flukier behind the front — but honestly, the rain and fog may make the call for you.

Tide & current

High water was around 10:00 AM (about 4.5 ft) and it drains down to a low near 3:40 PM — and that's a proper low (basically zero on the gauge), so give the shallow edges and the flats near the harbor margins a wide berth in the afternoon. Current inside the harbor is gentle: near slack around 11:15, then a light ebb building through the early afternoon (setting SSW, seaward). Out in the East Passage the ebb has real teeth (over a knot, running down-bay past Hog and Dyer Islands from about 1 PM). With the breeze crossing that outgoing current, expect some short, lumpy chop out toward the mouth in the afternoon — nothing scary, just don't be surprised by it if you poke out past Hog Island.

Good to know

Cool and damp today — air around 71–72°F, patchy fog, and a high (100%) chance of rain showers, so pack foul-weather gear and don't count on drying out. Visibility could drop in the fog, so keep a good lookout and consider staying where you know the water. Given the gusty potential behind the front, this is a life-jackets-on kind of day, and reef early or shorten sail if it's blowing over the low teens. If you want the best shot at a decent sail, head out mid-morning and plan to be back by early-to-mid afternoon — before the low tide, the strengthening ebb chop, and the northwest gusts all stack up. When in doubt, there's no shame in leaving this one for a nicer day.
