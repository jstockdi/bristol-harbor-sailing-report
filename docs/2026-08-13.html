Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Thu 13 Aug 2026 · Daysail

Daysail 10:00–18:00

A genuinely good sailing day — steady SSW breeze in the 10–15 kt range; get out mid-morning through mid-afternoon.

The day

This is a lovely day to go sailing — a proper, reliable summer southerly rather than a drifter. The live anemometers down at Newport and up at Conimicut are already reading a steady 12–15 kt from the SSW this morning, running noticeably stronger than the forecast models expected, so plan for a spirited but very manageable breeze rather than the light 6–7 kt the raw numbers suggest. Add sunshine and comfortable temps and you've got close to a perfect Bristol daysail — enough wind to have fun, not so much you'll be white-knuckling it.

Wind

Expect a steady SSW sea breeze for the day, filling right in from the East Passage over Hog Island and building through late morning. Realistically you're looking at a solid 10–15 kt with gusts into the mid-teens — a nice powered-up sail where a smaller boat or a nervous crew might want a hand on the mainsheet, but nothing extreme. The breeze veers gently through the day toward the W/SW as the afternoon wears on. The one soft spot to expect is the usual lee tucked in behind Poppasquash Point on the western shore — if you sail up into its shadow you'll find lighter, patchy air, so ghost through and pop back out into the pressure. The best window is roughly late morning to mid-afternoon while the sea breeze is fully established; it'll ease back later in the day.

Tide & current

High water was around 8:50 AM (4.5 ft) and it drains to a very low tide at 2:28 PM (about -0.2 ft) — that's a big minus tide, so give the shallow harbor edges and flats a wide berth through the early-to-mid afternoon, and mind committee-boat-shallow corners near shore. Current runs strongest through the harbor mouth by Hog Island and out in the East Passage; the tide turns to ebb (setting SSW, seaward) around midday — strongest in the Passage near 11:50 AM–1:00 PM (over a knot out there). Because that ebb sets into the SSW breeze, expect some short, choppy wind-against-tide waves around the harbor mouth and out in the Passage midday — nothing scary, just a heads-up so it doesn't surprise you. It eases to slack in the harbor around 2:15 PM.

Good to know

Warm and pleasant — 73–81°F, mostly sunny to partly sunny with just a small (under 30%) chance of a passing shower, so pack a light layer but expect a nice day. With gusts into the mid-teens it's worth having life jackets on for kids and less-experienced crew, and keep the sunscreen and water handy in the August sun. A good plan: head out mid-to-late morning to catch the building breeze, enjoy the peak of it through early afternoon, and aim to be back before things get very shallow around the 2:30 low and before the breeze softens later on. Have a great sail!
