Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Tue 01 Sep 2026 · Daysail

Daysail 10:00–18:00

A workable, breezy-ish day out of the ESE — go mid-morning before the rain arrives; carry a jacket.

The day

This is a sailable day with a caveat: there's a nice, steady breeze in the 8–9 knot range with gusts pushing the high teens, so it'll feel like a spirited-but-friendly daysail rather than a drifter. The catch is the sky — patchy fog early, mostly cloudy, and a real chance of rain moving through, so treat it as a "grab your window and keep an eye up" kind of day. Cool, gray, and breezy: good sailing if you dress for it and don't mind cutting it short if a shower rolls in.

Wind

The breeze is up and blowing from the ESE (unusual — this isn't your classic SW sea-breeze day), and it's already there this morning: the live gauges show a solid 10–14 knots up the bay, gusting into the mid-teens. Through the day it eases just a touch and slowly backs toward the ENE/NE, settling into a comfortable 8–9 knot working breeze with gusts poking near 18–20 in the puffs — enough to power you up nicely on a small boat, nothing you need to reef for. Models are in tight agreement, so you can trust the picture. Expect the usual soft patch tucked in behind Poppasquash Neck on the west shore where the land blocks an easterly, and slightly lighter air right down at the harbor mouth off Hog Island. The steadiest pressure sits up the harbor and out toward the East Passage. Best window is late morning into early afternoon, before the weather closes in.

Tide & current

High water is right at 11:17 AM (about 4.1 ft), dropping to a low around 4:55 PM (near 0.3 ft) — so the water gets thin late in the afternoon. Give the shallow edges near the harbor shores and any skinny spots off the points a wide berth as you head back in. Current in the harbor is gentle: it's flooding (setting NNE, up into the harbor) through late morning, goes slack around 12:30 PM, then ebbs SSW back out toward the Passage, peaking mid-afternoon. Out in the East Passage the flow is stronger (over a knot on the ebb) — worth knowing if you venture out past Hog Island. With the ebb running SSW against an ESE breeze, you may notice a little short chop building out toward the mouth and Passage in the afternoon; nothing alarming, just don't be surprised by the bumpier ride.

Good to know

It's a cool one — air only in the upper 60s (66–69°F) — under mostly cloudy skies with patchy morning fog, so bring layers and a waterproof jacket; rain is very much on the table today (high chance through the day). Keep an eye on visibility if the fog lingers early. With gusts into the high teens, it's a sensible day to keep life jackets on, especially with kids or newer crew aboard. Plan to head out mid-to-late morning to catch the best of the breeze and beat the wettest weather, and aim to be back by early-to-mid afternoon — that keeps you ahead of both the falling tide over the shallows and any rain moving in.
