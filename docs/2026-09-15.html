Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Tue 15 Sep 2026 · Daysail

Daysail 10:00–18:00

A mellow, sunny drifter — light N/NE wind under 8 kt; best chance for a sail is late morning into early afternoon.

The day

It's a gentle, easygoing day on the water — think relaxed family daysail rather than a spirited beat. A crisp, sunny September day with light air in the 4–6 kt range and gusts only nudging 8–9 kt, so you'll be ghosting along rather than heeling over. Perfect if you want a peaceful cruise, a picnic at anchor, or to teach someone the ropes; a little frustrating if you were hoping to be powered up. Bring patience and enjoy the sunshine.

Wind

Expect light air all day. It starts out of the N/NNE in the morning and slowly veers around toward the E (and further toward the SE out toward the East Passage) as the hours go on. The forecast averages a soft 4–6 kt, and the live anemometers back that up nicely — Newport at the Passage mouth is reading around 9–10 kt with a gust to 12, while Conimicut up-bay is a lighter 4 kt, so there's a bit more life the further S/seaward you get toward Hog Island and the Passage. Models and obs broadly agree, so this is a confident light-air call.

Your best window is roughly late morning through early afternoon (11:00–14:00), when there's the most to work with — after that it stays light and may fade. Watch for soft spots and holes in the lee of the Poppasquash shore (west side) on this northerly flow; the breeze tends to thin out and get patchy tucked in there, so stay out toward more open water if you want to keep moving.

Tide & current

High water is around 11:27 AM (about 4.0 ft) and it drops to a low near 4:54 PM (0.5 ft), so you'll have a good ebbing tide through the afternoon. Keep that low water in mind late in the day around the shallow harbor edges and any thin spots near shore — give them a wide berth as the water drops.

Current is gentle inside the harbor. At the harbor mouth by Hog Island the flow turns to a light ebb (setting SSW, seaward) after about 12:40 PM. Out in the East Passage the current runs noticeably stronger — an ebb building through the afternoon (peaking around 1 kt). In this light air it's really just something to be aware of: with the breeze light, the current can carry you more than the wind, so keep an eye on your drift near marks and moorings. There's no meaningful wind-against-tide chop today — it'll be flat and pleasant.

Good to know

A beautiful, clear early-fall day: sunny to mostly sunny, no rain (0%), and highs only in the mid-60s (64–68°F). Dress a layer warmer than midsummer — it'll feel cool out on the water once you're moving, especially in the morning and again as the sun drops. No thunderstorm risk to worry about. Sunscreen still matters even in the cool air.

An easy plan: head out mid-to-late morning to catch the best of the breeze around high tide, and aim to be back by late afternoon (5–6 PM) before the light fades and the tide gets low. Conditions are gentle enough that this is a very forgiving day — but keep life jackets handy as always, and enjoy the calm.
