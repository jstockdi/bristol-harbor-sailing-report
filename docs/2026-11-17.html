Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Tue 17 Nov 2026 · Daysail

Daysail 10:00–18:00

A crisp, mellow late-fall sail — light W/NW breeze, bright sun; go midday and be in before the early dark.

The day

This is a gentle, bundle-up-and-enjoy-it kind of day rather than a thrilling one. A light westerly off the land keeps the water flat and the harbor calm, with wall-to-wall sun and highs in the mid-50s. Think easy family daysail or a quiet solo potter — never powered-up, occasionally a touch drifter-ish, but pleasant and forgiving all afternoon. If you want a relaxed November outing, this fits the bill.

Wind

Expect a modest W to WNW breeze around 5–7 knots through the middle of the day, with occasional little puffs into the low teens — enough to keep you moving nicely without ever needing to depower. The direction drifts gradually from NW toward due W as the afternoon goes on. The breeze is pretty steady across the harbor and out toward Hog Island, so you won't find a dramatically better spot by sailing out — the sailing's much the same inside and out.

The best of it is roughly 11:00–14:00, when it's most consistent; after about 15:00 it starts to soften, so plan to be winding down by then. Live readings back this up — Conimicut up the bay is running a steady 7–8 kt while Newport at the mouth is lighter at 4–5 kt, and the forecast models all agree, so confidence is good.

One soft spot to expect: with the wind out of the W/NW, the water just off the E/SE side of Poppasquash Neck sits in the shore's wind shadow — you'll glide into a lull there and the breeze will pick back up (and nudge a bit) as you clear it. No surprise, just a quiet patch to sail around.

Tide & current

High water is right at midday (13:05, ~2.9 ft), so you'll have plenty of depth for your whole outing — nice and relaxed around the harbor edges and shallower corners. It falls away through the afternoon toward a low near 19:14, well after you should be back in.

Current is light and not a factor inside the harbor. Out at the harbor mouth off Hog Island it's a gentle flood (into the harbor) early afternoon, going slack around 14:46. The East Passage runs a bit more — a flood easing to slack around 14:23 — but it's modest. With the breeze from the west and seas a foot or less, there's no meaningful wind-against-tide chop to catch you out — it'll be smooth sailing.

Good to know

Bright and sunny all day, zero rain, highs around 55–57°F dropping toward 50 as the sun gets low — so dress in proper layers, hat and gloves welcome, and bring a windproof top since that westerly will feel cool over the water even in sunshine. Mid-November means early sunset (around 4:30), so treat that as your hard deadline: head out mid-morning to late morning and aim to be tied up by 15:30–16:00 while there's still good light and breeze. The air is light enough that life jackets are just good standard practice here rather than a rough-day necessity — but with cold water this time of year, keep them on and keep someone posted on your plan. Enjoy a lovely, peaceful fall sail.
