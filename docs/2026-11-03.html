Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Tue 03 Nov 2026 · Daysail

Daysail 10:00–18:00

A lovely late-season sail — steady SW breeze, sun, and a comfortable afternoon; best from late morning through about 4 PM.

The day

This is a really nice one to get out for — a classic autumn daysail with a steady southwesterly, plenty of sun, and no weather drama. The forecast models put the breeze in the gentle 7–8 kt range, but the real anemometers are already running stronger (Newport is reading 12–13 kt gusting to 17), so plan for a bit more punch than the numbers suggest. Call it a pleasant-to-spirited day: comfortable for a family cruise, with enough wind to keep a keelboat moving nicely and the odd gust to keep you honest.

Wind

Expect SW to SSW all day, roughly from 205–225°, filling nicely by late morning and holding right through the afternoon. On the water it should feel like a solid, enjoyable breeze — easing to pleasant in the lulls and briefly powered-up in the gusts (mid-teens out toward the mouth and East Passage). The seaward spots off Hog Island and into the Passage will have the cleanest, most consistent air. Your one soft patch is the usual one: tuck in close under the Poppasquash shore to the west and the point will steal your wind — expect a lull and some fluky angles there before it fills back in as you work clear. The breeze should stay up into the late afternoon and soften toward evening.

Tide & current

Low water is right at 9:00 AM (a skinny 0.6 ft), then the tide fills all morning to a high around 2:48 PM before ebbing again. With that early low, give the shallow harbor edges and shoals a wide berth first thing — there's more water everywhere by midday. Current in the harbor itself is gentle; the real flow runs through the gut by Hog Island and down the East Passage. The tide floods (up-bay) through early afternoon, then slackens around 3:30–4 PM at the mouth and starts ebbing (seaward, SSW) later in the afternoon. That late ebb runs against the SW breeze, so if you're out near the harbor mouth or the Passage toward 5–6 PM, expect a short, choppy slop to kick up — nothing alarming, just a heads-up so it doesn't surprise you.

Good to know

A bright fall day: partly-to-mostly sunny, highs around 67°F, dipping toward the low 60s, and essentially no rain (4% at most) and no thunderstorm concern. It'll feel cooler on the water than on shore this time of year, so layer up and bring a windproof shell — the SW breeze will have a cool edge. With gusts reaching the mid-teens, it's a good day to have life jackets on, especially for kids and anyone less steady. Aim to head out late morning once the breeze is established, enjoy the prime window through about 4 PM, and plan to be back at the dock before dusk (early November sunset comes quickly) and before that late-afternoon ebb chop builds. Have a great sail!
