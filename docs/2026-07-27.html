Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Mon 27 Jul 2026 · Daysail

Daysail 10:00–18:00

A lovely mellow-to-pleasant summer sail — sunny and warm, with the best breeze filling in through the early afternoon.

The day

This is a proper feel-good summer day on the water. Skies are sunny and clear, air in the mid-to-high 70s, and essentially no rain to worry about. The wind is on the gentle side — a soft morning that fills into a pleasant afternoon sea breeze — so think easygoing family daysail rather than a hang-on-the-rail thrash. Get out for the middle of the day and you'll have warm sun, flat-to-light chop, and enough breeze to keep things moving without ever feeling overpowered.

Wind

Early on it's a drifter — the live anemometers are reading just 2–4 knots out of the NW around mid-morning, so if you launch at 10 you may be ghosting along for a bit. Be patient: the classic SW sea breeze wants to fill through late morning into the afternoon, swinging around to come up from the S. Once it settles in, expect a nice, friendly 6–8 knots with occasional puffs into the low teens — enough for a relaxed reach without breaking a sweat. The sweet spot is roughly noon to mid-afternoon, when the breeze is most reliable.

A couple of gentle heads-ups: it'll generally be a touch livelier the farther S you go, out toward the Hog Island mouth and the East Passage, and lightest tucked up inside the harbor. Expect a soft, shifty patch in the lee E/SE of Poppasquash Neck (the west shore) while the wind is still westerly — the breeze fills back in as you clear its shadow. One note on confidence: the models disagree a little on just how much breeze reaches the seaward end (some keep it lighter), so treat the outer numbers as "hopefully" rather than "guaranteed" and enjoy whatever shows up.

Tide & current

High water was early (around 07:00), then it drops to a very low low around 12:19 (only ~0.5 ft) before building back to a healthy high near 19:30. That midday low is the thing to watch — give the shallow harbor edges and any skinny water off the shorelines a wide berth through late morning and early afternoon, and don't cut corners near marks or moorings.

Current is gentle inside the harbor and never a big deal for casual sailing here. At the harbor mouth off Hog Island it's a light ebb (running SSW, seaward) through late morning, going slack around 12:40 and staying quiet all afternoon. Out in the East Passage the flow is a bit stronger — a solid ebb SSW-bound easing to slack near 14:00. Nothing dramatic, but while the morning ebb is running against the building southerly you may notice a little short chop out toward the Passage — nothing a daysailer can't handle, just don't be surprised by it.

Good to know

It's a beautiful, safe-feeling day: sunny to mostly clear, 72–79°F, and effectively no rain or thunderstorm risk (1%). Bring water, sunscreen, and a hat — the sun will be strong on flat water with little cloud, and it's easy to cook out there without noticing. Life jackets on the little ones as always; the odd low-teens puff in the afternoon is very manageable, but a gust is always a good reminder. A nice plan: head out around late morning to catch the breeze filling, enjoy the prime early-to-mid-afternoon window, and aim to be back before evening as the wind softens toward sunset. Have a great sail!
