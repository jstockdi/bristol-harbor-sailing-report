Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Fri 27 Nov 2026 · Daysail

Daysail 10:00–18:00

A big, blustery SW day — fun for salty hands with a reef in, too much for a mellow family outing; mornings are only marginally tamer.

The day

Be honest with yourself before you rig up: this is a spirited-to-too-much day, not a lazy Thanksgiving-weekend drift. A solid SW blow is pumping up the bay — forecast winds of 20–25 knots gusting to 30, and the Conimicut anemometer is already reading low-20s with gusts to 25. If you're an experienced sailor itching for an exhilarating, well-reefed close reach, it'll be a blast. If you're thinking easy daysail with kids or guests aboard, this one's better admired from the dock with a hot drink. Add late-November water temps and it's a day to respect.

Wind

The breeze is up and here to stay — no waiting for a sea breeze to fill today. It's blowing SW across the whole harbor and East Passage, steady around 20 knots with real punch in the gusts (upper-20s to 30). It's powered-up to reef-it territory from the moment you leave the dock, holding right through the afternoon with only the gentlest easing late. Direction barely wanders — SSW veering a touch through the day — so at least it's honest about where it's coming from. Models and the live buoys agree closely (nice and confident), so plan for the full strength rather than hoping it's bluffing. Your one bit of relief: tuck in close under the Poppasquash shore to the west and you'll find a softer, flatter lee — a good spot to catch your breath, shake out or tuck in a reef, or just sail more calmly. Out past Hog Island and into the East Passage it's the opposite — a knot or two more wind and bigger, open water, so that's the zone to treat with the most caution.

Tide & current

High water is early at 09:28 (about 4.5 ft), draining to a low around 15:35 that goes slightly below datum — so give the shallow harbor edges and any skinny spots near Poppasquash a wide berth through the afternoon; there's less water than usual under you. The current ebbs SSW (seaward, down-bay) through midday, going slack at the harbor mouth around 14:23, and it runs noticeably harder out in the East Passage channel (over a knot early afternoon). The good news: today's SW wind and the ebbing current are pointed roughly the same way, so you won't get the nasty stacked-up wind-against-tide chop — the seas will be big and rolling but reasonably organized. Just know that an evening flood turns the current back up-bay (NNE), which would start to oppose the wind later — but that's past most daysail hours.

Good to know

Skies are mostly sunny with a few clouds and a 50% chance of a passing light shower, so pack a layer that sheds spray and rain. Air temps only reach the upper 50s (dropping toward the high 40s), and with the wind and cold water it'll feel much cooler out there — dress warm, dress for getting wet, and assume you'll be damp. This is absolutely a life-jacket-on, clip-in, tell-someone-your-plan kind of day given the gusts to 30 and the chilly water. If you do go, favor the morning into early afternoon when the light's best and the chop hasn't fully built, stay in the more sheltered water up the harbor and under the Poppasquash shore, and be back at the dock before the light fades — the wind isn't easing much and the cold sets in fast this time of year. When in doubt today, shorten sail early and keep it close to home.
