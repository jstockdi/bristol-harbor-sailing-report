Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Wed 19 Aug 2026 · 18:00–20:00

Race window 18:00–20:00

Steady SSW sea breeze 8–9 kt gusting 16; East Passage ebb (SSW) runs hard against the wind through the window, harbor mouth slacking ~19:03 — and low water lands at 19:20.

Wind

A textbook summer SSW sea breeze, filling steadily and evenly across the whole course. The five fixes cluster at 8–9 kt averaging, gusting to ~16, direction locked in the 204–208° band with essentially no gradient shift — inner harbor sits at 8.1 kt/200°, the outer fixes (South of Hog, East Passage) run 8.3–8.8 kt/206°. Only a very slight seaward edge in pressure: the East Passage / off-Hope-Island fix is the strongest at 8.8 kt, the inner harbor the softest. Model spread is tiny (0.1–0.8 kt across HRRR/ECMWF/GFS) — treat the strength and direction as high-confidence.

The live anemometers back the sea-breeze story: Newport (Passage mouth) reads S 191° building through ~10 kt, Conimicut up-bay SSE 167° peaking near 12 kt — both had backed/filled over the prior two hours as the breeze established. Earlier obs ran a touch under the model (0.79 ratio) but that was the light pre-fill morning; expect the breeze to hold or firm slightly into the evening, easing only near the end as the sun drops. Watch the western Poppasquash shore for a soft patch — the usual light lee sits to its E/SE.

Current

The tactical driver tonight. Two clocks:

  • Harbor mouth (Hog Island NW, ACT2171): a weak ebb (~0.3 kt, set SSW ~199°) is dying to slack at 19:03, then flood builds NNE up-bay after. So the first ~hour has a light seaward drain at the mouth, going near-slack by mid-window.
  • East Passage (Dyer Island W, ACT2156): a much stronger ebb, ~0.9 kt setting SSW ~216°, runs through nearly the entire window, not slacking until 20:15. This is the real flow.

With the wind blowing from the SSW and the ebb setting toward the SSW, wind and current oppose — expect short, steep wind-against-tide chop building in the East Passage and the entrance gut, easing as the Passage ebb fades toward 20:15.

Tide

Falling all through the window to a low of 1.02 ft at 19:20, right in the middle of racing. Flag shallow water: mind depth over shoal marks and near the harbor edges, and check committee-boat depth if the line is set toward the shallows.

Tactical call

Course-agnostic: Pressure and direction are near-uniform, so this is a current race, not a shift race. The lever is the East Passage ebb (SSW ~216°, ~0.9 kt) running the whole window against a steady SSW breeze. On any seaward leg, stay out of the mid-channel SSW set — when heading up-bay (N) work the shallower shore edges to duck the foul stream; when running seaward (S) the ebb is a friend, so ride the channel axis down. Inside the harbor and at the mouth the flow is weak and going slack ~19:03, so near-slack inner-harbor legs reward pressure over current — nudge toward the marginally stronger seaward breeze (Hog south / East Passage) when the course allows. And plan legs around the 19:20 low near shoal marks.

If the course is set: - N–S windward/leeward: with no E–W wind gradient, the cross-course lever is current, not shear — on the S beat into the Passage ebb, favor whichever edge keeps you off the strongest mid-channel SSW set; the ebb carries you down on the run. - J-hook out of the harbor: start in the near-slack inner harbor (weak dying ebb), then the hook into the mouth/Passage puts you into the stronger SSW ebb — expect it to sweep you toward the seaward mark; lay lines shift as you clear the gut. - Around Hog Island: each leg gets its own call — the mouth is going slack while the Passage side still ebbs hard SSW; carry the ebb on the seaward/southern legs, fight it up the shore on the return. - Out-and-back (light air): breeze is the priority — reach/run S toward the slightly firmer East Passage air; the ebb helps you out and you'll pay the chop and foul stream coming home.
