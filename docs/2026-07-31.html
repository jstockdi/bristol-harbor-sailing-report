Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Fri 31 Jul 2026 · Daysail

Daysail 10:00–18:00

A mellow, light-air day — pack patience and go out early afternoon when the southerly fills.

The day

This is a gentle one — a classic light-air summer daysail rather than a spirited rip. Winds sit in the drifter-to-easy-cruise range (around 5 knots, gusts under 10 in the harbor), so it's a lovely, low-stress day for a relaxed sail, a picnic aboard, or teaching someone the ropes. Just don't expect to hike out. The morning starts soft and a bit directionless as the leftover NW breeze fades and the sea breeze tries to organize itself, so the reward goes to whoever waits for the afternoon fill.

Wind

Early on, the breeze is light and out of the NW/W, and honestly a touch fluky — the live anemometers at Newport and Conimicut were only reading 2–3 knots this morning, even softer than the models suggested, so treat any before-noon plan as a drifter. Through midday the wind swings around to the S/SSW as the sea breeze sets up off the East Passage — that's your best window, roughly 1 to 5 PM, when things settle into a steady 5–8 knots. It's a nice, easy sailing breeze, nothing you'll need to reef for. Expect a bit more life the farther south you wander toward Hog Island and the East Passage (gusts nudging into the low teens out there), and a predictable soft spot tucked under the Poppasquash shore on the west side — if you park in there you'll go quiet, so give it a little room and stay out where the breeze can find you.

Tide & current

High water was at 9:39 AM, dropping to a notably low tide at 3:14 PM (about 0.25 ft) before filling again toward the evening high at 9:52 PM. That afternoon low is quite skinny, so mind the shallow edges and mudflats around the harbor fringes and off the points — favor the deeper middle if you're poking around near shore. Current stays gentle inside the harbor. At the mouth by Hog Island it goes slack around 11 AM, then a light ebb (running SSW, seaward) builds through the early afternoon before easing near 3 PM. Out in the East Passage the flow is stronger — up to about a knot of ebb around midday — and with the southerly breeze blowing against that outgoing current you may notice a little short chop out past Hog Island. Nothing alarming, just a heads-up so it doesn't surprise you.

Good to know

Beautiful summer weather: mostly-to-partly sunny, air in the mid-70s (74–77°F), and comfortable. There's a slight (~20%) chance of a passing shower or thunderstorm, so glance at the western sky now and then and have a plan to duck in if you see a cell building. Bring sun protection and water — mid-70s and sunny on the water adds up. With such light winds this is an easy, forgiving day, but life jackets on for the kids and anyone new is always the right call. Head out around midday to catch the sea breeze as it fills, and aim to be back before evening — both to stay ahead of any late-day storm risk and to avoid the skinniest water at the mid-afternoon low. Enjoy it out there.
