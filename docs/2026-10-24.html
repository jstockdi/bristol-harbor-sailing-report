Bristol Harbor · Rhode Island

Sat 24 Oct 2026 · Daysail

Daysail 10:00–18:00

A gentle, sun-soaked drifter — light all day, so go for the mellow sail and grab the best breeze before noon.

The day

This is a calm, pretty autumn day on the water — mostly sunny, crisp, and very light. Think peaceful family daysail or a lazy afternoon of ghosting along rather than anything spirited. The breeze is only running a couple of knots through most of the window, so if you love a quiet sail (or want to practice light-air trim) it's lovely; if you're after some heel and spray, this isn't the day for it. Bring patience, a thermos, and enjoy the quiet.

Wind

Light the whole way through. Early on there's a little more to work with — the Newport anemometer down at the East Passage mouth is reading around 5 kt out of the N, and up-bay at Conimicut it's a softer 4-ish from the NNW. Forecast and obs agree, so trust that this is genuinely a light day (models and the marine forecast both say ~5 kt or less). Expect a slow clock around: early northerly easing and then drifting toward the S/SW by late afternoon as the direction swings right through the day. Realistically it stays in the drifter range — 2 to 4 kt with occasional 6–7 kt puffs — so your best breeze is late morning into early afternoon before it goes soft toward evening. Watch for extra-glassy patches tucked under the Poppasquash shore to the west, where the land steals what little wind there is; stay out toward the middle of the harbor and down toward Hog Island to keep moving.

Tide & current

High water was early at 06:44, with low tide at 12:44 (basically 0.0 ft) — so around midday the shallow edges near the harbor fringe and any thin spots by the moorings will be at their skinniest. Give the shoreline and the shallows off the harbor mouth a wide berth near noon. Current is mellow and conveniently timed: at the harbor mouth it's a gentle ebb easing to slack around 12:12, then filling back as a light flood later in the afternoon. Out in the East Passage the flow is a bit stronger (ebbing SSW mid-morning, slack ~13:24) but still modest. With such light wind there's no real wind-against-tide chop to worry about — the water should stay glassy-smooth all day (waves a foot or less).

Good to know

Gorgeous fall weather: mostly sunny to clear, highs around 58°F, lows near 48°F, and no rain in the picture (0%). Dress for the season — it'll feel cool out on the water even in the sun, so layers, a windbreaker, and a warm hat go a long way, and the low-October sun can still catch you, so bring sunglasses and sunscreen. No gusty or life-jacket-urgent conditions today, but wear them anyway — chilly water makes that an easy call this time of year. Head out late morning to catch the best of the breeze around the slack-current window, and plan to be back before the wind fades toward evening so you're not ghosting home in the dusk chill. Easy, pleasant day — enjoy it.
