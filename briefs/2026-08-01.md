## The day
This is a lovely, low-key day to get out on the water — think easy family daysail rather than a hang-on-and-hike affair. A gentle southwesterly sea breeze builds through the afternoon into the 6–8 knot range with occasional puffs to the mid-teens, so you'll always have enough to move nicely without ever feeling overpowered. Add mostly-sunny skies and comfortable air in the high 70s and you've got a classic Bristol summer sail. Just don't expect much wind first thing — it's a slow starter.

## Wind
It's quiet early: the up-bay anemometer at Conimicut Light was reading only 2–4 knots out of the south around 8 AM, so mid-morning will be drifter conditions if you push off right at 10. Be patient — the sea breeze fills through late morning and settles into a pleasant SSW-to-SW flow of about 6–8 knots by early afternoon, with the strongest, steadiest air out toward the harbor mouth and down into the East Passage (a knot or two more breeze the farther south you go). Puffs to ~15 knots will keep it interesting without demanding a reef on most boats. The models are in tight agreement on this one, so it's a confident forecast. Your best window to be out is roughly **noon to 4 PM**. Expect a soft, shifty patch tucked in behind the western Poppasquash shore — if you find yourself parked over there, nudge out toward open water to pick the breeze back up.

## Tide & current
High water is at **10:18 AM** (about 3.6 ft) and low water at **3:52 PM** (nearly dead low at 0.3 ft). That afternoon low is worth noting: give the shallow edges and flats near the harbor margins and Poppasquash shoreline a wide berth mid-afternoon, and keep an eye on your depth if you're poking near the shore. Current inside the harbor is gentle — near slack around 11:47 AM at the mouth, then a mild ebb draining seaward (SSW) into the early afternoon. It's much stronger out in the East Passage channel (over a knot on the ebb), so if you venture down past Hog Island in the afternoon with the breeze from the south against that outgoing tide, you may feel a little short, choppy slop. Nothing alarming for a daysail — just know it's coming so it doesn't catch you off guard.

## Good to know
Comfortable and summery all around: air 76–80°F, mostly-to-partly sunny, and only a slim (~16%) rain chance — no thunderstorm worries in the data. Bring sunscreen, water, and a hat; the sun will be strong on the water even with a light breeze. A good plan is to head out around **11:30–noon** as the breeze fills, enjoy the best of it through the afternoon, and aim to be back by **4–5 PM** as things ease and the tide bottoms out. It's light enough that this is a relaxed, everyone-welcome day — though with those mid-teen puffs it never hurts to keep life jackets handy, especially for kids and less-experienced crew. Enjoy it!
