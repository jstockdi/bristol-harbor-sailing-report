## The day
This is a proper easy-going daysail day. Think sunshine, warm-but-not-baking air, and a gentle SW breeze in the 7–8 kt range with the odd puff into the low teens — enough to keep sails full and the boat moving without ever feeling like a handful. It's a mellow family kind of day: nobody's getting overpowered, and the biggest decision is whether to pack a second sandwich. If you like a little more zip you'll want the afternoon, when the sea breeze is at its steadiest.

## Wind
It starts out light — the anemometers were only reading a few knots early this morning (Newport ~4 kt, and it's even lighter up-bay at Conimicut), so if you head out first thing expect drifter conditions. The classic SW sea breeze should fill in through late morning and settle into a comfortable **7–8 kt from the southwest** for the afternoon, gusting into the low-to-mid teens out toward Hog Island and the East Passage. That's the sweet spot: nicely powered-up, easy to handle. The **best window is roughly noon to late afternoon**, and the models are in good agreement, so you can trust that picture.

Expect the usual soft patch tucked in behind **Poppasquash Neck** (the west shore) — the breeze goes light and shifty there, so don't be surprised if you park up in its lee. You'll generally find a touch more consistent air out toward the **harbor mouth and south of Hog Island**, where the sea breeze comes in cleanest.

## Tide & current
High water is early (**5:51 am**) and again in the evening (**6:22 pm**), with a **low at 11:18 am** — and it's a low one, just **0.7 ft**. Around late morning keep an eye on the shallow edges near the harbor fringes and any skinny water off the points; give the shoreline a wider berth than usual until the flood starts filling back in.

Current is light and friendly inside the harbor. Out at the **harbor mouth**, the last of the morning ebb (setting SSW/seaward) fades to slack around **11:25 am**, then the afternoon flood gently builds (setting NNE, up into the harbor). In the **East Passage** the flow is a bit livelier — a solid morning ebb, slack near **12:37 pm**, then flood in the afternoon. None of it is strong today, and with a light SW breeze you shouldn't see any nasty wind-against-tide chop — just a little slop in the Passage during the morning ebb if you venture out that way.

## Good to know
Gorgeous conditions overhead: **sunny to mostly sunny**, highs around **73–75°F**, and essentially **no rain** (2% chance) — no thunderstorm worries at all. It'll feel warm on the water with all that sun, so bring water, a hat, and sunscreen; the sea breeze will keep you comfortable but it's easy to underestimate the sun out there. Nothing here calls for heavy weather gear, though a few gusts into the teens are reason enough to have life jackets on hand (and on the kids). A nice plan: **head out around late morning to catch the breeze filling**, enjoy the steady afternoon, and aim to be **back by the 6:22 pm high** to make docking easy.
