## Wind

A **NNE gradient northerly (~030°)**, not a sea breeze — it pours off the Bristol/Poppasquash land, so expect it **shifty and puffy**. Sail the oscillations and stay in phase rather than banking on one side. Race-window model averages run **10–14 kt with gusts to 25–26 kt**; the fix spreads are small (0.3–2.9 kt), so the regime is high-confidence.

**Pressure gradient:** strongest at the **harbor mouth (Hog Island Shoal, ~13 kt)** and out in the **East Passage (~14 kt)**; softest **under the western Poppasquash shore (~10.5 kt)** — its lee sits to the E/SE on a northerly — and **south of Hog Island (~9.6 kt)**.

**Live obs are running punchier than the models — trust the harder number.** At 15:36 Conimicut Light read **22.7 kt avg, gusting 25.9, NNE (030°)** and Newport read **16.5 kt avg, peak 20** with direction veering from 029° toward **ENE (057°)**. That up-bay strength and the seaward veer say: rig for the top of the range and watch for a right-hand (toward the E) edge on the seaward legs.

## Tactical call

Play the phases, keep a clear lane, and don't commit to a corner. **Pressure lives at the harbor mouth and out into the East Passage**; steer clear of the **Poppasquash lee (E/SE of the west shore)** and the **soft patch south of Hog Island**. Current is small and slackening, so treat it as a tie-breaker: the fading **NNE flood** faintly favours the up-bay (N) lane early, going neutral by ~19:15–19:38. Guard against the wind-against-current chop early in the window.

**If the course is set:**

- **Out-and-back** — not a light-air night; there's plenty of breeze, so **get seaward to the East Passage pressure** and ride the shifts home.
