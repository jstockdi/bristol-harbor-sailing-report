## The day
This is a crisp, sunny October sailing day with plenty of air — fun if you like a lively ride, a bit much for a mellow family potter. A gusty **northerly** is blowing down the bay at a solid 12–16 kt with gusts punching into the high 20s and low 30s, so it lands in the **spirited-to-marginal** range depending on your boat and crew. If everyone aboard is comfortable with a heel and a bit of spray, you'll have a great time; if you were hoping for a lazy drift, maybe save it for a lighter day.

## Wind

It's out of the **N**, funneling straight down the East Passage, and it holds through the afternoon before easing a touch toward evening. Expect **powered-up** sailing most of the day with gusts worth respecting, so **put a reef in** before you leave the dock if your boat likes one. Forecast and obs agree closely, so trust the breezy picture. For a calmer ride, tuck up toward the **northern/eastern end of the harbor** and in the **lee of Poppasquash Neck** (the west shore) — on a northerly its shadow softens the breeze in the harbor's upper corners. Out past **Hog Island** and into the **East Passage** it's a few knots stronger and wave-ier, so that's the zone for the thrill-seekers, not the faint-hearted.

With the **N wind blowing against the up-bay flood later in the day**, expect **steeper, choppier little waves out near the Hog Island mouth and in the Passage** in the afternoon — nothing alarming, but it'll feel bouncier out there than inside the harbor.

The upside is a **beautiful sky** (partly-to-mostly sunny) with **no rain in the forecast** and zero thunderstorm worry. With gusts into the 30s, this is a genuine **life-jackets-on, clip-the-mainsheet-hand day** — reef early, keep an eye upwind for the darker puffs rolling down the bay, and don't be shy about staying inside the harbor if the Passage looks too feisty. A good plan: **head out late morning once you're bundled up, enjoy the brightest, breeziest hours, and aim to be back at the dock by late afternoon** before the chop builds and the light starts to go.
