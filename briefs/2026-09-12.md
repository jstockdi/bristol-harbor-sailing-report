## The day
This is a soft, easygoing day on the water — think mellow family daysail rather than anything spirited. Wall-to-wall sun, mild air in the low 70s, and a light breeze that never really loads up: forecast averages sit around 5 knots with the odd puff to 10. It's a lovely afternoon for a relaxed sail, a picnic at anchor, or teaching someone the ropes — just set your expectations to "ghost along" rather than "rail down," and bring patience (and maybe a paddle for the calm patches).

## Wind
Morning starts genuinely light — the live anemometers are reading only 2–4 knots so far, a touch under what the models advertised, so don't rush out at the dock at 10. Expect a thin easterly/southeasterly early that slowly swings around toward the S through the afternoon as the sea-breeze tries to fill up the East Passage from Hog Island. Your best window is roughly **noon to mid-afternoon**, when you'll get the steadiest (if still modest) 5–8 knots and the odd gust into double digits to keep things fun. It stays a drifter-to-light day throughout — never powered-up. Watch for the usual soft spot **tucked in behind the Poppasquash shore to the west**, where the land shadows the breeze; if you park there, edge back out toward the middle of the harbor or down toward Hog Island where there's a hair more air.

## Tide & current
High water is at **9:14 AM** (about 4.6 ft) and it drains to a **low around 2:58 PM** — and this is a proper low, right down to about zero. That's the one thing to keep in mind: give the shallow harbor edges and any skinny spots near shore a wide berth through the early-to-mid afternoon, and don't cut corners around the flats. Current is gentle inside the harbor — near slack around 10:30 AM, then a light ebb building (setting SSW, seaward) through the early afternoon. Out in the **East Passage** the ebb has more muscle (around a knot near midday), so if you wander out past Hog Island you'll feel it carrying you seaward. With such light wind there's no real wind-against-tide chop to worry about — it'll stay smooth and friendly.

## Good to know
Beautiful weather for it: sunny to partly cloudy, highs of 73°F, and **no rain in the forecast** — zero percent, no thunderstorm worries. It's mild enough that you'll want a layer for the shade and the ride home as the afternoon cools. With plenty of sun on the water, pack sunscreen, a hat, and water. No gusty conditions to fret over today, but life jackets on the kids as always. A nice plan: **head out around noon** to catch the best of the breeze, and aim to be **back by late afternoon** before the wind fades out with the day and the tide bottoms out. Enjoy it — this is a classic gentle Bristol Harbor afternoon.
