## The day
This is a low-key, bundle-up-and-enjoy-the-foliage kind of sailing day rather than a thrilling one. Expect light northerly air in the single digits with only occasional puffs, cool autumn temperatures, and plenty of sun. If you're after a relaxed family daysail or a quiet solo poke around the harbor, it's lovely; if you want speed and heel, you'll be a little under-powered. Think of it as a drifter with just enough breeze to keep you moving.

The live anemometers at Newport and Conimicut back that up (both reading mid-single-digits around midday, obs and forecast in good agreement), so there's no hidden extra breeze waiting out in the East Passage this time. The NWS calls N winds 5–10 kt this afternoon, **diminishing toward 5 kt** — so the best of what little there is comes earlier. Aim to be out around **late morning into early afternoon**; by mid-to-late afternoon it may go glassy. With this northerly, expect a soft, fluky patch tucked in behind **Poppasquash Neck (the west shore)** — if you park there, drift out a bit to find the clearer air. A touch more consistent breeze lives down toward the **harbor mouth and south of Hog Island**, though nothing dramatic today.

## Good to know
A crisp, pleasant fall day — highs around **62°F**, dipping toward the mid-50s, mostly sunny early and clouding up a bit later, and **no rain in the window** (showers hold off until Sunday). Dress for the season: layers, and bring a windbreaker since it'll feel cool on the water even in sunshine. Nothing here is rowdy enough to demand extra caution, but a life jacket is always smart, especially with that cooler October water. **Head out late morning** to catch the best of the breeze, enjoy a relaxed few hours, and plan to be **back by mid-afternoon** before the wind fades and that very low tide bares the shallows. Fair winds and enjoy the quiet.
