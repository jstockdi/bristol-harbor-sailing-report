## The day
This is a lazy, easygoing day on the water — think light-air cruising, not a spirited rail-down romp. A warm, sunny high-pressure day with a soft WSW breeze in the 5-knot range means you'll ghost along nicely under full sail, but you won't be hiking out. It's a lovely one for a relaxed family daysail, a picnic-at-anchor, or teaching someone the ropes. If you want a bit more life in the sails, aim for the early-to-mid afternoon when the sea breeze gets its best (still modest) push.

## Wind
Expect a light **WSW breeze** all day, averaging around **5 knots** with occasional puffs to 10-11 in the strongest patches — squarely drifter-to-just-sailable territory. It was very light at dawn (the Newport and Conimicut anemometers read only 2-4 kt from the south this morning), and the models and obs agree on that, so don't rush out early hoping for pressure. The **best window is roughly noon to 4 PM**, when the afternoon sea breeze adds what little muscle the day has to offer. A touch more air tends to sit out toward **Hog Island and the harbor mouth** than deep inside; the softest, most fickle patches will be tucked in the **lee to the east/southeast of the Poppasquash shore**, so if you park there, drift out toward more open water to find breeze. Keep your sails full and light and you'll have a pleasant time.

## Tide & current
It's a big tidal swing today. **Low water is early — 8:04 AM at just 0.3 ft**, which is genuinely skinny, so give the shallow harbor edges and any thin spots near the shore a wide berth first thing. Things fill back in to a comfortable **high of 3.9 ft at 3:23 PM** — nice deep water for your afternoon sail — before dropping again to low at 10:16 PM. Current in the harbor is gentle and largely a non-issue; it's near slack at the mouth around **8 AM and again about 4:25 PM**, with a mild flood (setting up-bay/NNE into the harbor) filling in through the early afternoon. The flow is friskier out in the **East Passage** than inside the harbor, but at these breeze strengths you won't see meaningful wind-against-tide chop — just calm, easy water. Nothing to surprise you.

## Good to know
Gorgeous summer weather: **sunny to mostly clear, 81-86°F, and only about a 10% chance of any rain** — essentially a bluebird day. That said, it'll be hot with strong sun, so bring **water, sunscreen, and a hat**, and remember light-air days can cook you sitting still. There's no gusty or threatening weather in the picture, so this is a low-stress outing — but keep life jackets aboard and handy as always, especially with kids. A good plan: **head out around late morning to catch the building breeze, enjoy the fullest sailing through mid-afternoon, and be back at the mooring comfortably before evening** as the wind fades with the day. Enjoy it.
