## The day
This is a really nice one to get out on. A warm, mostly sunny September day with a friendly WSW sea breeze around 8–12 knots — enough to sail properly and keep things fun without being a handful. There are gusts into the high teens and low 20s to keep you honest, so it sits right between a mellow family cruise and a spirited sail: settled and easy most of the time, with the odd puff that'll heel you over and put a grin on your face.

## Wind
Expect a steady breeze from the WSW that slowly veers toward the W through the afternoon (roughly 245° swinging to 280° by evening). Averages sit near 10 knots — a nice, powered-up daysail — with gusts that can nudge 18–20, so be ready to ease the sheet in the bigger ones. The live anemometers this morning read a touch lighter (5–7 kt at the moment) but were gusting into the teens over the past couple of hours, so the breeze is filling in on schedule and the models are in good agreement — you can trust a solid sailing wind by midday. **Late morning through mid-afternoon is your best window.** Watch for a soft, gentler patch tucked in behind the Poppasquash shore (the western side of the harbor) when the wind's out of the west — if you go quiet there, ease out toward the middle of the harbor or down toward Hog Island to find the pressure again.

## Tide & current
High water is early (07:42) and again in the evening (20:06), with **dead low around 13:27** — and it's a proper low (essentially 0 ft), so give the shallow edges near the harbor fringes and any skinny water around the island tips a wide berth in the middle of the day. Current is gentle inside the harbor itself. Down at the harbor mouth and especially out in the East Passage the tide runs harder: it's ebbing (setting SSW, seaward) through late morning, then goes slack around 13:10 at the mouth (a bit after 14:00 in the Passage) before the flood builds up-bay in the evening. Since the wind and the morning ebb are both heading roughly the same seaward way, don't expect much nasty chop — just know the water's moving under you near Hog Island Shoal, so factor a little set into your course if you're poking out into the Passage.

## Good to know
Gorgeous conditions overall: highs around 83°F, lows near 75, mostly sunny skies and only an 8% chance of a passing shower — basically a rain-free, sunscreen kind of day. It'll be warm on the water, so bring water, a hat, and sun protection. With gusts touching the high teens/low 20s, it's a good day to keep life jackets on, especially for kids and less-experienced crew. A good plan: **head out late morning to catch the breeze filling in, enjoy the strong afternoon window, and aim to be back by late afternoon** before the wind eases toward evening. Mind that midday low tide on your way in and out of the shallows. Have a great sail!
