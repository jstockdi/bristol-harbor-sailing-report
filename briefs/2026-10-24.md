## The day
This is a calm, pretty autumn day on the water — mostly sunny, crisp, and very light. Think peaceful family daysail or a lazy afternoon of ghosting along rather than anything spirited. The breeze is only running a couple of knots through most of the window, so if you love a quiet sail (or want to practice light-air trim) it's lovely; if you're after some heel and spray, this isn't the day for it. Bring patience, a thermos, and enjoy the quiet.

## Wind

Forecast and obs agree, so trust that this is genuinely a light day (models and the marine forecast both say ~5 kt or less). Realistically it stays in the **drifter** range — 2 to 4 kt with occasional 6–7 kt puffs — so your best breeze is **late morning into early afternoon** before it goes soft toward evening. Watch for extra-glassy patches tucked under the **Poppasquash shore to the west**, where the land steals what little wind there is; stay out toward the middle of the harbor and down toward **Hog Island** to keep moving.

Easy, pleasant day — enjoy it.
