## The day
This is a good day to sail if you like a bit of muscle in the breeze — a spirited, powered-up northeasterly rather than a lazy drifter. Expect a steady 13–15 kt with gusts into the low-to-mid 20s, cool early-fall air, and lots of sun. It's a fun one for confident crews and well-found boats; if you're taking the family out or sailing something small and tender, treat it as a reef-first, keep-it-conservative day. The one wrinkle: it's a northerly, so it'll be shifty and puffy rather than smooth.

## Wind
The breeze is already up and blowing this morning — the real anemometers are reading 15–18 kt from the NE/NNE, Conimicut up-bay gusting to 24 — and it holds strong and fairly consistent right through the afternoon (models are tightly clustered around 14–15 kt, so this is a confident call). Direction stays out of the **NE/NNE** all day, backing just slightly. In plain terms: **powered-up, occasionally reef-it in the gusts**, especially out toward Hog Island and the East Passage where it blows a knot or two harder. Because it's coming off the land to the **N**, expect it to be puffy and to wander around a bit — stay alert on the mainsheet. The calmest patch will be tucked in under the **Poppasquash shore on the west side**, where the neck throws a lee — a nice spot to duck in for a breather or to reef, then expect the breeze to fill back in and swing a touch as you clear its shadow. Any part of the day works wind-wise; **mid-afternoon** is the sweet spot for warmth and full sun.

It smooths out considerably once the ebb eases toward early afternoon — another reason mid-afternoon is the nicer time to be out.

Cool and bright: highs only around **59–63°F**, mostly-to-partly sunny skies, and just a **low (~23%) chance of a passing shower** — dress in layers and bring a windbreaker, it'll feel brisk with that breeze on the water.
