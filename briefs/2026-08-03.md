## The day
This one's on the heavy end of fun and leaning toward "maybe not today." A solid SSW breeze is already up — the real anemometers are reading the high teens at Newport and the high **20s with 30 kt gusts** up at Conimicut — and the sky comes with a 90%+ chance of rain showers and some morning fog. If you love a spirited, over-canvassed sail, a well-found boat with an experienced crew and a reef in could have a blast on the flatter water inside the harbor. For a mellow family daysail or anyone in a small centerboarder, this is a marginal-to-too-much day; there's no shame in leaving it on the mooring and watching from the porch.

## Wind
Steady and strong out of the **SSW** (about 185–195°) all day, filling straight up the East Passage over Hog Island. Forecast averages sit around **15–16 kt**, but the live obs are already running hotter, and with showers around you should expect **squally gusts well past 25–30 kt**. That's powered-up to reef-it territory, not a gently-drifter afternoon. The models all agree closely (low spread), so treat the strength as high-confidence — this isn't going to fizzle out on you. Your calmest water and softest air will be tucked up under the **western Poppasquash shore**, which throws a lee on this southerly; nice for a breather, just know the breeze snaps back to full strength the moment you clear its shadow toward Hog Island and the open Passage. If you go, the least-bad window is the earlier side before showers build.

## Tide & current
High water is around **11:44** (3.7 ft) and it drains to a **low near 17:13** (just 0.4 ft), so keep an eye on the **shallow harbor edges and any thin spots off the points late afternoon** — easy to nudge the bottom near low water. Current is modest at the harbor mouth (flood peaks mid-morning, slack about 13:04, a gentle ebb after), but the **East Passage runs much harder** — over a knot of ebb building through the afternoon (slack ~12:41, then ebbing toward 14:40). Here's the heads-up: the SSW wind blowing against that seaward ebb will kick up **short, steep wind-against-tide chop** in the Passage this afternoon. It'll be wet and bouncy out there — another reason to keep it inside the harbor if you go at all.

## Good to know
Air's warm but not hot — **mid-70s** — under fog and rain-shower skies, so you won't roast, but you'll likely get wet. **Rain is more likely than not (over 90%)**, with patchy morning fog cutting visibility early. Given the gusts, this is absolutely a **life-jackets-on, reef-in, buddy-boat** kind of day — dress for spray, skip it if you're solo or new to heavy air. If you're set on going, head out earlier before the showers thicken and be back on the mooring well before the late-afternoon low and the building Passage chop. When in doubt today, the smart call is a rain check.
