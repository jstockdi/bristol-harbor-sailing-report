## The day
Yes — go. This is a classic easy-going October daysail: a soft, friendly southwesterly in the 5–8 kt range, wall-to-wall sun, and almost no chance of rain. It's a mellow family day rather than a spirited one — you'll be ghosting along comfortably, not hiking out. The live anemometers are actually reading a touch stronger than the models expected (Newport's been running 7–10 kt), so you may get a few more honest puffs than the forecast suggests, which is a nice bonus on a light day.

## Wind
Look for a gentle SW breeze through the day, averaging around 5–6 kt with occasional puffs to the low teens — comfortable "nice sailing" territory, leaning drifter in the lulls. The direction slowly veers from SW toward the W as the afternoon goes on, so expect the breeze to clock a bit right over your shoulder across the day. Newport out at the East Passage mouth has already been the breeziest spot (around SSW 8–10 kt), so if you want the most reliable air, drifting down toward Hog Island and the Passage tends to pay. The classic soft patch to watch is the lee tucked in behind **Poppasquash Neck** on the western shore — on this W-leaning breeze it'll go glassy close under that shore, so don't get parked there. Best window to be out is roughly **late morning through mid-afternoon**, before the breeze starts to fade toward evening.

## Tide & current
High water was early (around 08:11), with a **low tide at 14:13** that's actually below chart datum (about −0.2 ft) — so give the shallow edges near the harbor fringes and any skinny spots a wide berth right around early-to-mid afternoon. Out in the East Passage the current ebbs (setting SSW, seaward) through the morning, easing to slack around **14:39** before gently flooding back up-bay. With the light SW/W breeze running over an ebbing current earlier in the day you might feel a little short chop near the harbor mouth and off the island tips, but nothing a casual sailor will find unsettling in this gentle air — just a heads-up so it's not a surprise.

## Good to know
A crisp, pretty fall day: sunny skies, highs around **64°F** and dipping toward the high 40s, with essentially no rain (1%) and no thunderstorm concern. Dress in layers — it'll feel cooler out on the water and in the breeze than it does on the dock, especially as the sun gets lower late afternoon. Winds stay light enough that it's relaxed sailing, but a light jacket and a hat go a long way this time of year. A good plan is to head out mid-to-late morning, enjoy the best of the breeze and sun through early afternoon, and aim to be back by late afternoon as the air softens and the chill sets in. Life jackets on as always — easy day to just enjoy being out there.
