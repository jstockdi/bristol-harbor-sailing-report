## The day
Honestly? This one's on the marginal side for a fun sail. None of that is dangerous for a well-found boat and a warm crew, but it's not a relaxing family drift either — think short, purposeful outing in foul-weather gear rather than a lazy afternoon. If you want to get out, the first half of the window is your friend before the wet weather and building breeze settle in.

Plenty fine for a quick, spirited sail — just not a day to linger.
