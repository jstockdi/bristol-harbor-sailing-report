## The day
This is about as good as September gets — sunny, dry, comfortable, and a friendly
south-southwesterly filling the harbor. It's a proper sail-your-boat breeze:
enough to lean on and make good speed, but nothing that'll have you white-knuckled.
Call it a mellow-to-spirited daysail, fine for the whole family with a hand or two
on the sheets. The models are in tight agreement (barely a knot of spread), so you
can trust the picture.

## Wind
Expect a **SSW breeze right through the day**, averaging around **8 kt inside the
harbor and 9–10 kt out toward Hog Island and the East Passage**, with occasional
gusts into the mid-teens. That's a nice, powered-up-but-relaxed feel — no reef
needed for most boats, though a puffy gust to 15–17 kt off Poppasquash Point is
worth a glance now and then. The live anemometers already back this up: Newport at
the Passage mouth and Conimicut up-bay are both reading high-single-digits SSW,
matching the forecast nicely. It's a touch **breezier the farther out you go** —
Hog Island and the East Passage carry the most pressure — while the inner harbor is
the calmest. Expect the usual **soft patch tucked in under the Poppasquash shore on
the west side**; if you slide in there and go slack, just nose back out toward the
middle to find the breeze again. No sign of it dying off, so any time from mid-morning
to late afternoon is a good window.

## Tide & current
**High water is around 12:14 (a modest 3.6 ft)**, with **low water at 17:36 (0.8 ft)**
just as the afternoon winds down. Keep an eye on the shallow edges near the harbor
fringes and mudflats late in the day — give the shore a little extra room as the
tide drops toward that evening low. Current is gentle at the harbor mouth (flooding
gently up-bay this morning, slack around 13:26, then a light ebb building down-bay in
the afternoon). Out in the East Passage the flow is stronger, but since the ebb runs
SSW — the same direction the breeze is blowing — wind and tide are working together,
so you shouldn't run into any nasty stand-up chop. Comfortable water all around.

## Good to know
Beautiful conditions: **sunny to mostly sunny, highs around 70°F**, and essentially
**no rain (3% chance)** — a bluebird fall day. It'll feel cooler out on the water than
on land, so bring a layer, and don't skip the sunscreen even with the milder temps.
Nothing threatening in the sky, but with gusts poking into the mid-teens it's a good
day for everyone to have life jackets on and sheets uncleated in the puffs. Head out
whenever suits you late morning onward, and aim to be back before that evening low
around 17:30 so you're not threading the shallows in fading light. Enjoy it.
