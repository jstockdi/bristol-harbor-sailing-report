## The day
This is a proper good one — a classic mellow-to-lively late-August daysail. A steady SSW sea breeze around 8–10 kt fills in over the afternoon, warm sun, almost no rain risk, and comfortable temps in the upper 70s. It's gentle enough for a relaxed family cruise but with just enough puff (gusts creeping toward the high teens) to keep it fun if you want to hike out a little. Nothing marginal here — go enjoy it.

## Wind
Expect a textbook southerly sea breeze building up the East Passage from the S/SSW (around 205–215°). Mornings start soft — the live anemometers were reading only 5–7 kt on average through late morning — so if you head out early it'll feel like a gentle drifter to a light-and-easy sail. Through the afternoon it settles into a nice, sailable 8–10 kt, with occasional gusts nudging the high teens, so a fresh puff now and then. The **best window is roughly noon to late afternoon**, when the breeze is most reliable and even across the harbor. Model agreement is strong and the forecast lines up well with what's actually blowing, so you can trust this one.

Watch for the usual soft spot tucked in behind **Poppasquash Neck** (the west shore) — the breeze goes light and patchy in its lee, so if you drift in there and stall, ease out toward the middle of the harbor or down toward **Hog Island** to find pressure again. You'll generally find a touch more breeze out toward the harbor mouth and the East Passage than deep inside.

## Tide & current
High water is at **09:48** and again late at 22:07; low water is at **15:33** — and it's a very low one (near 0.1 ft), so give the harbor edges, shallow flats, and any skinny spots off Poppasquash a wide berth through the mid-to-late afternoon. Don't cut corners near shore around 3–4 PM.

For current: it's near slack at the harbor mouth (**Hog Island**) around 11:11, then an **ebb sets seaward (SSW, ~199°) building through early afternoon** and easing to slack near 15:10. Out in the **East Passage** that ebb is noticeably stronger — over a knot around midday. With the SSW breeze blowing against the outgoing ebb out toward the Passage, you may notice a bit of short, bumpy chop in the afternoon, especially near the harbor mouth and off the island tips. Nothing alarming for a casual sail, just don't be surprised by the slop — and if you want flatter water, stay tucked up inside the harbor.

## Good to know
Beautiful conditions: highs around 79°F, lows near 72°F, partly-to-mostly sunny skies, and only about a 5% rain chance — no thunderstorm worry today. Bring sunscreen, water, and a hat; that late-summer sun is strong on the water. With gusts occasionally in the high teens, it's a good day to have everyone in life jackets, especially kids and anyone new to sailing.

Head out around late morning if you like it gentle, or aim for early afternoon to catch the breeze at its best. Plan to be back before the wind and light start to fade toward evening — a return by 5–6 PM makes for an easy, relaxed finish to the day. Enjoy it out there.
