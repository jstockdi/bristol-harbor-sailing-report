## The day
This is a lovely, low-key day to get out — warm September sun, mostly clear skies, and a gentle **northwesterly around 7 knots** that never really builds into anything demanding. Think easy family daysail: enough breeze to keep moving and trim a sail without anyone hanging on. The one thing to keep in mind is that a NW breeze blows off the land, so it'll be a touch **puffy and shifty** rather than the smooth, steady SW sea breeze you get on a classic summer afternoon. Nothing to worry about — just expect the odd lull and a little swing in direction.

That's comfortable "nice sailing" territory the whole window; no reefing, no drifting. This morning's live readings (Newport and Conimicut both showing 4–6 kt) are just the light early hours filling in, and the forecast is confident, so plan on a pleasant, consistent afternoon. **Best time to be out is late morning through mid-afternoon**, when the sun's up and the breeze is most settled. With the breeze this light there's no meaningful wind-against-tide chop to catch you out — just know that if you wander out into the Passage late in the day, the ebb will be trying to carry you seaward, so leave yourself an easy trip home.

## Good to know
Gorgeous conditions: **highs 72–77°F**, **sunny to partly cloudy**, and **no rain in the forecast** — a proper bluebird fall day. Bring sunscreen, water, and a light layer for when the breeze picks up on the water (it always feels cooler out there than on the dock). Nothing here needs foul-weather planning. A good rhythm would be to **head out mid-to-late morning**, enjoy the steadiest breeze through the early afternoon, and **be back by late afternoon** before that East Passage ebb firms up and the day cools. It's gentle enough for an easy sail, but with those puffy NW gusts it's always smart to have **life jackets on hand** — and worn by kids and anyone who wants them. Enjoy it out there!
