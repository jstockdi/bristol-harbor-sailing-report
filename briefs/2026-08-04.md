## The day
It's a lovely, low-key day to be on the water — warm, sunny, and calm, the kind of afternoon made for a relaxed family daysail rather than a spirited rail-down blast. The breeze is light throughout, so set your expectations for gentle sailing and maybe a bit of ghosting along in the soft patches. If you want the best of what little wind there is, aim for the afternoon and point yourself toward the more open water south of Hog Island and into the East Passage, where it's a touch stronger.

## Wind
Expect a light southwesterly all day — think **drifter to barely-nice**, in the 5–6 kt range with occasional puffs to 9–12 kt out toward the mouth. The morning starts especially soft: the live anemometers at Newport and Conimicut are only reading 2–3 kt right now, actually lighter than the models suggested, so don't rush out early expecting to sail. The sea breeze should nudge things along through early-to-mid afternoon as it fills up from the S/SSW, so **roughly noon to 3–4 PM is your best window** to catch steady air. You'll find a little more pressure the farther south and seaward you go (around Hog Island and into the East Passage); expect a genuinely soft spot in the lee tucked under the Poppasquash shore on the west side of the harbor, so don't be surprised to park there. Direction drifts from SW toward more southerly as the day goes on — nothing dramatic, just a gentle clock around.

## Tide & current
High water is right around **12:32 PM** (about 3.8 ft), then the tide drains all afternoon to a **low near 6:01 PM** (about 0.6 ft). That means shrinking depth toward evening — give the shallow edges near the harbor fringes and any thin spots a wider berth as the water drops, especially if you're out late. Current is modest at the harbor mouth (flooding gently up-bay this morning, slack around 1:47 PM, then a light ebb building seaward). Out in the East Passage the flow is livelier — ebb picks up to roughly a knot by mid-afternoon, setting SSW toward the sea. With the wind this light, don't expect any nasty wind-against-tide chop; at most you'll notice the current gently nudging your track, so just keep half an eye on it if you drift out into the Passage.

## Good to know
It's a beauty: **sunny to mostly sunny, 74–80°F, and no rain in the forecast** — a proper summer day. With this much sun and heat, bring water, sunscreen, and a hat; the light air won't cool you off much. No thunderstorm risk to worry about today, and the gentle breeze means no drama — though it's always smart to have life jackets aboard and on the kids. A relaxed plan: **head out around late morning-to-noon** to catch the filling sea breeze, enjoy the warm afternoon, and aim to be **back before that evening low around 6 PM** so you've got water under you coming home. Easy, pleasant, and no rush.
