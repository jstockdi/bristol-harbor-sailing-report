## The day
This is a lovely day to be on the water — bright early-autumn sunshine, dry air, and a friendly northeasterly in the 8-knot range. It's got a touch more life than a dead-calm drifter, with gusts reaching the mid-teens, so call it a pleasant, mildly spirited daysail rather than a lazy float. Enough breeze to keep the boat moving nicely without ever feeling like a handful, and the whole window is usable.

## Wind
The breeze is already in this morning out of the **NE** and holds through the day, slowly veering toward the **E** by late afternoon. Expect a comfortable **7–9 kt average** with **puffs into the mid-teens** — nice sailing, the kind where a lightly powered-up boat is having fun, not fighting anything. The live anemometers back this up: Newport's reading around 9 kt at the Passage mouth and Conimicut up the bay closer to 10–11 with gusts near 14, and the forecast agrees with what's actually blowing, so you can trust it.

One northerly quirk to expect: a NE wind comes off the land, so it'll be a little **shifty and puffy** rather than perfectly steady — nothing dramatic, just stay awake on the sheet. Look for a **softer, patchy lee tucked in behind Poppasquash Neck (the west shore)** where the breeze goes light and swirly; if you want cleaner, more consistent air, stay out toward the middle of the harbor and down toward **Hog Island** and the **East Passage**, where it fills in best.

## Tide & current
Low water was early (around 06:24), so the tide's **filling all morning** to a comfortable **high near 2:00 PM** — plenty of depth through the day, no low-water worries during your sail. A second low doesn't come until well after dark.

Current is light and friendly inside the harbor. Through midday it's **flooding (gently setting up-bay, into the harbor)**, which runs roughly with the NE wind — smooth going. It goes slack early-to-mid afternoon, then **turns to ebb (setting seaward, SSW) around 4:30–5:00 PM**. That ebb runs noticeably harder out in the **East Passage** than inside, and with the wind still out of the NE/E you can get a little **short wind-against-tide chop** out past Hog Island late in the day. Nothing alarming for a casual sail — just expect it to feel a bit bouncier out in the Passage after about 4 PM.

## Good to know
Gorgeous fall conditions: **sunny to mostly sunny all day, no rain at all**, highs around **68°F** and cooler in the morning near the upper 50s. That water-and-wind combo means it'll feel brisk once you're moving, so **bring a warm layer and a windbreaker** — you'll want it, especially heading home into the afternoon breeze. Sun's lower this time of year but still worth sunscreen and sunglasses off the water glare.

A good plan is to **head out late morning** once the day warms and the breeze is well established, enjoy the steady air through early afternoon, and aim to be **back by late afternoon** before the ebb kicks up that chop out by Hog Island. With puffs reaching the mid-teens, **keep life jackets on** for kids and anyone less steady on their feet — easy call on a day this nice. Have fun out there!
