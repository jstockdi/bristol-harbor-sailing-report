## The day
This is a lovely early-autumn day to get out — a genuinely fun, spirited sail rather than a lazy drifter. A steady breeze out of the **N/NNE** holds all day at around 10–14 knots, enough to keep you moving nicely with the occasional gust to remind you to pay attention. It's on the cool side (low 60s) and mostly sunny, so grab a layer and enjoy it. Nothing marginal here: not too light, not too much — just a solid, satisfying daysail.

## Wind
The breeze is a northerly today, blowing down over the Bristol and Poppasquash land from the **N/NNE**, which means it'll feel a touch **shifty and puffy** rather than glassy-smooth — expect it to breathe up and down and swing a bit through the day. Average is a comfortable **10–12 knots**, with gusts poking toward **18–20**, so you'll be nicely powered up and hiking in the puffs but never overwhelmed. The live anemometers agree with the forecast (Newport reading ~12 kt NNE, Conimicut ~14 kt up the bay), so confidence is good.

You'll find a little more push out toward the **harbor mouth by Hog Island** and into the **East Passage** (12 kt-ish), and slightly softer air tucked up inside. The classic soft spot on a northerly is in the **lee of Poppasquash Neck** — if you sail close under the **west shore**, expect the breeze to go light and patchy and then fill back in as you clear its shadow. Best window is really the **whole day**, but early afternoon gives you the sunniest skies to go with the breeze.

## Tide & current
Low water is right at the start of your window (**10:02 AM, only ~0.9 ft**), building to a high of ~3.0 ft at **4:47 PM**. With that low tide first thing, give the shallow edges of the harbor and any known thin spots near shore a wide berth in the morning — there's plenty of water by afternoon.

Current is gentle inside the harbor and only really shows itself near the **harbor mouth off Hog Island** and out in the **East Passage**. It runs slack around **10 AM** at the mouth, then floods (sets **NNE, up into the harbor**) through the afternoon. Since the wind is also out of the NNE, that afternoon flood runs **against the breeze** out in the Passage — so if you venture past Hog Island later in the day, don't be surprised by a bit of short, choppy **wind-against-tide** slop. It's nothing dramatic in this breeze, just a heads-up so it doesn't catch you off guard.

## Good to know
Cool and pleasant: highs only around **65°F**, lows near 61, so dress in layers — a fall day on the water feels chillier than on land. Skies are **mostly sunny to partly cloudy** with essentially **no rain** (4% at most) and no thunderstorm worries, so it's a clean, bright day. With gusts reaching the high teens to 20, it's a sensible day to **keep life jackets on**, especially with kids or newer crew aboard.

Head out mid-morning once you've cleared the low-tide shallows, enjoy the sunniest stretch through the early afternoon, and aim to be back by late afternoon around the 4:47 PM high — you'll have had a full, satisfying day of breeze. Have fun out there!
