Nothing a casual sailor needs to lose sleep over.

The sky starts **partly sunny** but trends to **mostly cloudy with a growing chance of rain showers** (up to about 50%) as the afternoon wears on. Keep an eye to windward and have foulies handy.

**Best plan:** head out late morning to catch the brighter, drier part of the day, enjoy a few relaxed hours, and aim to be back on the mooring by mid-to-late afternoon before the showers arrive. The breeze is friendly enough that it's an easy day, but with gusts into the teens it's always smart to have life jackets on the littlest crew.
