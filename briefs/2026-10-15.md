## The day
This is a pretty good autumn daysail if you dress for it — a brisk, sunny northerly morning that mellows as the afternoon goes on. The breeze is the shifty, puffy kind that comes off the land to the north, so expect lively patches between the lulls rather than a steady drone. Think spirited-but-manageable early, softening toward a relaxed drifter by late afternoon. The main catch is a cool day and a chance of showers sliding in later, so this is a "go in the morning" kind of day.

## Wind
It's already up and blowing as the window opens — the real anemometers out at Newport read around 12 kt and up the bay at Conimicut 15–17 kt with gusts into the low 20s, so the morning is genuinely breezy. The direction is **northerly (N, easing toward NE)**, straight down over the Bristol and Poppasquash land, which makes it gusty and a little oscillating — nice sailing if you stay alert on the sheet. Forecast and obs agree well, so you can trust the picture: a **powered-up 10–15 kt late morning, gusts near 17–20**, then gradually **softening to an easy 5–10 kt** through the afternoon.

Your **best window is roughly 10:30 to 2:00** while the breeze still has body. The classic soft spot on a northerly is **tucked under the Poppasquash shore on the west side** — the land throws a lee to its east and southeast, so you'll find a calm patch and the wind bending as you sail out of its shadow. For steadier pressure, stay out in the more open water toward **Hog Island and the harbor mouth to the south.**

## Tide & current
High water is right at midday (**11:35, ~3.5 ft**) and it drops to a **low around 17:04 (0.7 ft)** — so keep an eye on the shallow edges near the harbor and shoreline late in the afternoon as the water pulls down. Current is gentle at the harbor mouth (well under half a knot), turning from flood to **ebb just before 1 PM**, after which it sets **seaward to the SSW**. Out toward the **East Passage** the ebb is stronger — up to about 0.9 kt in the early-to-mid afternoon. That ebb runs *against* the northerly breeze, so if you venture out past Hog Island after 1–2 PM expect a bit of short, choppy slop where wind meets tide — nothing dramatic in a keelboat, just a heads-up so it doesn't surprise you.

## Good to know
It's a cool fall day — **highs only around 57–61°F** — so bring layers and a windbreaker; it'll feel colder out on the water in that northerly. Skies start **sunny and clear**, which is lovely, but **rain showers become likely later** (chance climbing toward ~70% into the evening), so plan to be back at the dock before the sky turns. With gusts into the high teens and low 20s in the morning, it's a **life-jackets-on, hands-on-the-sheet** kind of breeze early — great fun, just stay attentive. Simple plan: **head out mid-to-late morning** to catch the best wind, and **be back by mid-afternoon** ahead of the softening breeze, the building ebb chop, and any showers.
