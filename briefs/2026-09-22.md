## The day
This is a good day to go sailing, with a fun, spirited edge to it. A steady northerly is filling the bay at a solid 11–14 knots with gusts pushing 20, so it's less a lazy family drifter and more a hands-on, get-a-little-spray-on-you kind of day — great if you like a boat that's leaning into it, a touch much if you're looking for flat and mellow. The models are in tight agreement and the real anemometers back them up, so you can trust the breeze will be there. Just dress for autumn: it'll be cool, breezy, and bright.

## Wind
The wind is out of the NNE (roughly 20–30°) and it's already established — no waiting around for a sea breeze today. Expect a comfortable 11–14 knots across most of the day, powered-up but manageable, with gusts to around 20 that'll keep you honest. Being a northerly off the land, it'll be a bit shifty and puffy rather than smooth and steady, so keep a hand on the sheet. There's more consistent pressure out toward the harbor mouth and down by Hog Island (13–14 kt at Newport and Conimicut right now), and it eases a touch tucked up inside the harbor. Look for a soft, gusty patch in the lee to the E/SE of the Poppasquash shore — the breeze goes light and puffy there before filling back in as you clear the point. Best window is really anytime from late morning through late afternoon; it holds nicely all day.

## Tide & current
Low water lands at about 10:59 (only ~0.7 ft) and high comes in the evening at 17:38, so the morning is your shallowest stretch — give the harbor edges and any skinny spots a wide berth until the tide builds back through the afternoon. Current runs strongest through the entrance gut by Hog Island and down in the East Passage; inside the harbor it's gentle. The mouth is near slack around midday (11 a.m.–noon), then the tide turns to flood and pushes up-bay (NNE) through the afternoon. Heads-up: that afternoon flood runs against the NNE wind, so expect some short, steeper chop around the harbor mouth and out toward Hog Island — nothing alarming, just a bit lumpy, so ease out there rather than being caught off guard.

## Good to know
Cool and bright: highs only around 61–63°F, with a mix of sun and clouds and essentially no rain (3% chance) — no thunderstorm worries today. It'll feel brisk on the water with that breeze, so bring a windbreaker or spray layer and warm gloves; it's easy to get chilled in the wind even under sunshine. With gusts near 20, this is a life-jacket-on day, especially for kids or anyone in a smaller boat. Head out mid-to-late morning once you've cleared the lowest tide, and aim to be back by late afternoon while the light and warmth hold. A fun sail — just sail it with respect for the puffs.
