The lever is the **East Passage ebb (SSW ~216°, ~0.9 kt)** running the whole window against a steady SSW breeze. On any seaward leg, **stay out of the mid-channel SSW set** — when heading up-bay (N) work the **shallower shore edges** to duck the foul stream; when running seaward (S) the ebb is a friend, so **ride the channel axis** down. And plan legs around the **19:20 low** near shoal marks.
