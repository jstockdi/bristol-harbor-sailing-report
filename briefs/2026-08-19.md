## The day
This is a proper easy-going summer daysail — sunny skies, low 80s, and a gentle
southwesterly filling in over the afternoon. Mornings will be near-drifter light,
so let the breeze build before you cast off. Once the sea breeze settles into a
steady 7–9 knots you'll have a relaxed, no-drama sail — nothing that'll overpower
a family crew, just enough to keep things moving nicely. Think mellow, not
spirited.

## Wind
It starts out very quiet: the Newport and Conimicut anemometers are only reading a
couple of knots this morning, and honestly the models may even be a touch
optimistic for the early hours. Expect a real drifter until the sea breeze wakes
up. Through late morning into the afternoon the classic SW breeze fills up the
East Passage and over Hog Island, settling around **7–9 knots from the S/SW** with
occasional puffs into the low teens — comfortable "nice sailing" territory, never
reef-it. It gently **veers toward the SW** as the afternoon goes on.

The best window to be out is roughly **noon to 5 PM**, when the breeze is most
reliable. You'll find the steadiest air out toward **Hog Island and the harbor
mouth**; tuck up under the **Poppasquash shore (west side)** and you'll hit a soft,
patchy spot where the breeze goes light in the lee — fine to know if your sails
suddenly go quiet over there. Models are in tight agreement on strength, so you can
trust the gentle-breeze picture.

## Tide & current
High water is at **1:43 PM** and the next low is late, at **7:20 PM**, so you've got
plenty of water under you through the whole sailing window. There's an earlier low
at 6 AM, but that's long before you'd be out. Nothing shallow to worry about
midday — just be mindful of the harbor edges as the water drops toward evening.

Current is light and friendly inside the harbor. At the mouth it goes slack around
**2:54 PM**, with a gentle flood setting up-bay (into the harbor) before that and an
ebb heading seaward after ~5 PM. Out in the **East Passage** the flow is a bit
stronger — up to about ¾ knot — but with the breeze staying southerly and soft,
you shouldn't run into any nasty wind-against-tide chop. Just expect the water to
help or nudge you a little as you head out past Hog Island.

## Good to know
Gorgeous conditions: **mostly sunny, 76–81°F, and essentially no rain** (2% chance)
— no thunderstorm worries today. It'll be warm in the sun, so bring water, a hat,
and sunscreen. With winds staying gentle, this is an easy day for all levels; life
jackets on the little ones as always, but you won't need to brace for big gusts.

Simple plan: **head out around late morning to noon** once the sea breeze fills,
enjoy the steady afternoon air, and aim to be **back by early evening** as the
breeze softens and the water starts dropping. A perfect day for a relaxed sail.
