## The day
This is a gentle, low-key day on the water — think flat calm with the occasional whisper rather than a proper sail. The breeze is sitting around 3–4 knots just about everywhere, under sunny-to-partly-cloudy skies and a surprisingly mild November feel. If you love a quiet drift with a hot drink and no agenda, you'll enjoy it; if you want to actually move, you may find yourself motoring or waiting. It's a light-boat, patient-sailor kind of afternoon — totally relaxed, no drama.

## Wind
The morning starts almost glassy out of the N/NE and gradually swings around through the day to a light S/SE by afternoon — classic for a calm fall day. All our forecast fixes agree closely (barely half a knot between the models), so confidence in "it's light" is high. One encouraging note: the live anemometers are actually reading a touch stronger than the models (Newport around 4 kt SE, Conimicut around 3 kt S at midday), so you may get a bit more to work with than the numbers suggest — but still firmly in **drifter** territory, never powered up. The best window is roughly **midday into mid-afternoon**, once the southerly fills in and the sun's had time to work. Expect the usual soft, glassy patch tucking under the **Poppasquash shore to the west** — stay out toward the middle of the harbor and down toward **Hog Island** to the south, where there's marginally more air moving.

## Tide & current
Low water lands right at the start of your window — **10:35 AM, and it's a very low one (about 0.2 ft)** — so give the shallow edges and mud flats a wide berth early, especially along the harbor fringes; stick to deeper water until the tide builds back toward the **4:44 PM high**. Current is light and friendly today: the harbor mouth off Hog Island is near slack through late morning, turns to a gentle flood (up-bay, into the harbor) from around 3:20 PM. Out in the **East Passage** the flow is a bit livelier but still modest. With the breeze this soft there's no wind-against-tide chop to worry about — the water will stay calm and glassy, so just mind that early low tide and you're set.

## Good to know
A lovely mild day for late November — highs around **62°F**, lows near 53°F, lots of sun early with clouds filling in later, and essentially **no rain risk** (3% at most). Dress warmer than the air temp suggests, though: it's always cooler out on the water this time of year, so bring layers, a windbreaker, and something warm for your hands. No gusts to speak of, so conditions are very forgiving — but with chilly water, keep life jackets on as always. A good plan: **head out around late morning to midday** to catch the best of the breeze and the rising tide, and aim to be **back by late afternoon** as the light fades early and the air cools quickly once the sun drops. An easygoing one — enjoy it.
