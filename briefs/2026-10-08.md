## The day
This is a crisp, sunny October sailing day with real breeze behind it — not a lazy drifter. A west-to-northwest wind is already running harder than the models first guessed, so plan for a **spirited, powered-up sail** rather than a mellow family potter. If your crew enjoys heeling over and a bit of spray it'll be a treat; if you're after something gentle, this one's on the lively side. Dress warm, keep hands on the sheets in the puffs, and you'll have a gorgeous, clear-sky afternoon on the water.

The live anemometers back this up — Conimicut up the bay is already reading a solid 15 knots gusting 18 from the WNW, running stronger than the forecast, so trust the firmer end of that range. For steadier pressure, more open water toward **Hog Island and the harbor mouth** will feel cleaner.
