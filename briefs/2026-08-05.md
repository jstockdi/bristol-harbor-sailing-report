## The day
This is a proper easygoing day on the water — sunny, warm, and a soft southwesterly that never climbs past a friendly single-digit breeze. Nothing about it is intimidating: it's a mellow family daysail, ideal for a relaxed cruise around the harbor or a poke down toward Hog Island. The only thing to plan around is timing, since the morning starts nearly calm and the breeze takes a while to wake up.

## Wind
Right now the anemometers are barely stirring — Newport and Conimicut are reading only 1–4 knots out of the S/SSW — so an early launch will likely be a drifter. Expect the classic Bristol pattern: the southwesterly sea breeze fills in through late morning and settles into a pleasant **8 knots or so from the SSW**, with occasional puffs into the low teens. That's a nice, gentle sailing breeze all afternoon — enough to move along comfortably without ever feeling overpowered.

**Best window: roughly 12:00 to 17:00**, once the breeze is established. The forecast is rock-solid on this one (all the models agree within a knot), though the very light morning means don't rush out at 10:00 expecting to sail — give it until midday.

For soft spots, the usual one applies: tuck in close under the **Poppasquash shore (the west side of the harbor)** and you'll find a lee where the breeze goes light and patchy. If you want steady air, stay out in the open middle of the harbor or head down toward the harbor mouth and Hog Island, where the breeze comes in cleanest.

## Tide & current
Tides today: **low around 06:04**, **high at 13:24**, and the next **low at 19:04**. You'll have plenty of water under you through the whole sailing window, but keep an eye on that evening low if you're poking near the shallow edges of the harbor late in the day — favor deeper water toward dusk.

Current inside the harbor is gentle and won't bother you. Out at the harbor mouth off Hog Island, a light flood (setting up-bay to the NNE) runs into the early afternoon, going slack around 14:30, then a soft ebb (SSW, seaward) builds later. Down in the **East Passage** the flow is stronger — an ebb around 16:00 can run close to a knot to the SSW. With the breeze also from the SSW, that afternoon ebb sets up a mild wind-against-tide chop out in the Passage, so if you venture that far and the ride gets bouncy late in the day, that's why — nothing alarming, just a little short and steep.

## Good to know
Gorgeous summer weather: **sunny to mostly sunny**, highs around **81°F**, and essentially **no rain** (1% chance) — no thunderstorm worries today. That means sun and heat are your main concern, so bring water, sunscreen, and a hat, and expect it to feel warm out there with the light air.

A simple plan: **head out around noon** once the sea breeze has filled, enjoy the sweet spot through mid-afternoon, and aim to be **back by 17:00–17:30** before the breeze softens and the evening low water sets in. Conditions are gentle enough that life jackets are just good practice rather than a necessity, but keep them handy in the puffs — and enjoy what looks like a beautiful day on Bristol Harbor.
