## The day
This is a calm, easygoing autumn day on the water — perfect for a relaxed family sail, not a day to go looking for thrills. A soft northerly is in charge, averaging only about 6 knots with occasional puffs into the low-to-mid teens, so expect drifter-to-light conditions with the odd gust to keep you honest. It's cool and mostly cloudy with a chance of patchy fog, so dress for fall, but with almost no rain risk it's a pleasant window to potter around the harbor and out toward Hog Island if the breeze cooperates.

## Wind
Light northerly all day — think **N early, slowly backing toward the NW** by late afternoon. Numbers are steady and the models all agree (barely a knot of spread), so this is a confident forecast: **roughly 5–10 knots of actual sailing breeze with gusts to 15–16**. That puts most of the day in the "nice and gentle" range with brief powered-up puffs; nothing you'll need a reef for.

The breeze is a touch firmer out at the **harbor mouth and around Hog Island (to the south)** than tucked up in the inner harbor, so if you want the best of it, drift that way. Being a northerly off the land, it'll be a little **shifty and puffy** — fun if you enjoy playing the puffs, just don't count on it staying perfectly steady. Expect a **soft, patchy lee under the Poppasquash shore to the west**, so if you park there and go nowhere, nose back east toward open water to find pressure. Heads-up that it may go genuinely light and fickle at times (the Newport anemometer at the Passage mouth is reading only a couple of knots this afternoon, while up-bay it's closer to 9–10), so patience may be rewarded.

## Tide & current
**High water was around 09:29** (about 4.4 ft) and the tide draws down to a **low near 15:10** — and that's a proper low, almost dead-bottom. Give the **shallow edges of the harbor and the flats off Poppasquash a wide berth** through the early-to-mid afternoon so you don't find the mud.

Current is gentle right where you'll be: the **harbor mouth goes slack around 10:47**, then builds to a mild **ebb (setting SSW, seaward) through the early afternoon** before easing again near 14:54. Out in the **East Passage** the ebb runs stronger — up to about a knot setting SSW down-bay around midday — so expect a little more push out there. With the **northerly blowing against that seaward-running ebb**, you may notice a bit of light wind-against-tide chop near the mouth and in the Passage early afternoon; nothing dramatic in this breeze, just a slightly lumpier feel so you're not caught off guard.

## Good to know
Cool fall sailing: **air around 58–67°F**, mostly cloudy with some partial sun and a chance of **patchy fog**, and only about a 14% rain chance — so pack a warm layer and maybe a light shell, but you're unlikely to get wet from the sky. Keep an eye out if fog rolls in and slow down/keep a sharp lookout if visibility drops. No thunderstorm concern today.

A good plan: **head out late morning** once the harbor-mouth current goes slack and the breeze is at its steadiest, enjoy the gentle northerly, and aim to be **back by mid-to-late afternoon** before the low tide tempts you toward the shallows and the breeze softens off. Life jackets on for the kids as always, and with those occasional teens-of-knots puffs it's a sensible day to keep them handy for everyone. Enjoy it out there!
