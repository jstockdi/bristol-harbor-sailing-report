## The day
This is a lovely day to go sailing — a proper, reliable summer southerly rather than a drifter. The live anemometers down at Newport and up at Conimicut are already reading a steady **12–15 kt from the SSW** this morning, running noticeably stronger than the forecast models expected, so plan for a **spirited but very manageable breeze** rather than the light 6–7 kt the raw numbers suggest. Add sunshine and comfortable temps and you've got close to a perfect Bristol daysail — enough wind to have fun, not so much you'll be white-knuckling it.

## Wind

Realistically you're looking at a solid **10–15 kt with gusts into the mid-teens** — a nice powered-up sail where a smaller boat or a nervous crew might want a hand on the mainsheet, but nothing extreme. The breeze **veers gently through the day toward the W/SW** as the afternoon wears on. The one soft spot to expect is the usual **lee tucked in behind Poppasquash Point on the western shore** — if you sail up into its shadow you'll find lighter, patchy air, so ghost through and pop back out into the pressure. The **best window is roughly late morning to mid-afternoon** while the sea breeze is fully established; it'll ease back later in the day.

Warm and pleasant — **73–81°F**, mostly **sunny to partly sunny** with just a small (under 30%) chance of a passing shower, so pack a light layer but expect a nice day. Have a great sail!
