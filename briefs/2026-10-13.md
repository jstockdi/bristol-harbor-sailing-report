## The day
This is a lovely, low-key October daysail — the kind of day for a relaxed cruise rather than a white-knuckle rip. A gentle breeze in the high single digits holds all day, so you'll have steady, manageable sailing with nothing scary. The morning starts gray and damp with a bit of drizzle, but it clears toward mostly sunny by afternoon, so if you can wait out the early murk you'll get the best of it. Call it a mellow family daysail with just enough breeze to keep you moving.

## Wind
Expect a steady, friendly 7–9 kt most of the day with occasional puffs — nice sailing, never overpowered. The breeze starts out of the **E** in the morning and slowly swings round through the **SE** to a soft **southerly** by late afternoon (the live anemometers at Newport and up at Conimicut were already reading SSE–S around 6–8 kt midday, right in line with the forecast, so this is a confident call). The best window is **early-to-mid afternoon**, once the drizzle lifts and the sun helps the southerly settle in.

## Tide & current
High water is right at **10:10 AM** (about 4.1 ft) and it drains to a **low around 3:47 PM** (just 0.3 ft) — so the harbor edges and shallow flats are at their thinnest in the mid-to-late afternoon. Give the shallow margins near the shore and any skinny spots a wide berth around that low. Current is gentle inside the harbor, but it picks up through the **gut at Hog Island Shoal** and runs noticeably stronger out in the **East Passage**, where the ebb sets **SSW (seaward)** and peaks around midday near a knot. With the breeze out of the S and the ebb running against it through early afternoon, you may feel a little short, bouncy chop out by the harbor mouth and in the Passage — nothing alarming, just a heads-up so it doesn't surprise you. Things ease toward slack around **3:30 PM** at the mouth.

## Good to know
Comfortable fall air — highs around **73°F**, dipping toward the low 60s, so bring a layer for when the sun's behind clouds or the breeze finds you. Rain chance is low (under 10%) with just some patchy early drizzle, clearing to mostly sunny later — a genuinely pleasant afternoon. Breeze is light enough that this is relaxed sailing, but keep life jackets handy for the occasional puff into the teens, especially with kids aboard. A nice plan: let the morning gray burn off and **head out around noon or early afternoon**, enjoy the clearing skies and settling southerly, and aim to be back by **late afternoon** as the light fades and the water draws down toward low tide. Fair winds!
