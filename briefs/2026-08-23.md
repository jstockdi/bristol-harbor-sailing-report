## The day
This is a gentle one — a mellow family daysail, not a spirited rip. Winds sit in the drifter-to-light range most of the day (averages around 3–4 knots with occasional puffs to the low teens), so think easy pace, good company, and no pressure to hustle. It's a lovely day to potter out, practice light-air trim, or just anchor off Hog Island for a swim. Skies are a mix of partly sunny and cloudy with a chance of a passing shower, so keep an eye up — but nothing here should keep you off the water.

## Wind
Early on it's a light ENE breeze — you can see it in the live readings this morning (Newport around 4 knots, Conimicut a touch friskier near 7). Through the day the wind gradually swings around clockwise toward the S, which is the classic sea-breeze pattern trying to set up off the East Passage. Your best window to be out is the **early-to-mid afternoon**, when that southerly has the best chance to fill and give you steadier, slightly stronger air — the seaward spots (down by Hog Island and into the East Passage) will always have a knot or two more than up inside the harbor.

Honestly, plan for drifter-to-light "nice" conditions most of the day rather than powered-up sailing. One soft-spot heads-up: expect a hole and some fluky air in the lee tucked in behind the Poppasquash shore (the west side of the harbor) — if you park there, nose back out toward the open water to find pressure. Confidence on exact strength is a little loose today (one model wants 6–7 knots, the others closer to 3–4), so take any extra breeze as a bonus.

## Tide & current
Low water is at **10:22 AM** and it's a proper low (under a foot), so if you're heading out mid-morning give the shallow harbor edges and any skinny spots near the shorelines a wide berth. The tide then floods all afternoon up to a **high around 5:35 PM**, so depth gets more forgiving as the day goes on — nice timing for an afternoon sail.

Current is light and won't push you around much: the harbor mouth by Hog Island goes slack around **10:30 AM**, then a gentle flood builds through the afternoon (setting up-bay into the harbor). Out in the East Passage the flow is a bit stronger, but still modest today. With such light wind there's no real wind-against-tide chop to worry about — it'll be smooth going.

## Good to know
Comfortable temps in the low-to-mid 70s, so a pleasant day on the water — bring a light layer for the cloudier stretches. There's roughly a 1-in-3 chance of a passing rain shower, so a splash jacket is a smart pack, and glance to the west now and then for anything building. Sun will be in-and-out, but don't skip the sunscreen and water. With the breeze this light, gusts aren't a concern — but life jackets on the kids (and everyone, really) are always the right call. A relaxed plan: head out late morning to early afternoon to catch the filling southerly, and aim to be back around the evening high tide near 5:30 PM for an easy return.
