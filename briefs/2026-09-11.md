## The day
This is a lovely, low-stress day to be on the water — warm, sunny, and light. Think mellow family daysail rather than a spirited breeze: 5–7 knots most of the day with gusts only nudging into the low teens. Nobody's getting overpowered, so it's a great day for a relaxed cruise, a picnic sail, or teaching someone the ropes. The one catch is that it's genuinely light, so if you like to feel the boat heel you may spend some of the afternoon ghosting along.

## Wind
The breeze is a soft northwesterly to start — offshore off the Bristol and Poppasquash land — and slowly backs toward the west and WSW through the afternoon. Strength sits in the **drifter-to-nice** range: around 5–6 kt with occasional puffs to 10–12. The live anemometers this morning back that up nicely (Newport and Conimicut both reading roughly 5–8 kt out of the NNW), and the models are in good agreement, so there are no hidden surprises. Because this is an offshore, land-driven breeze rather than the usual afternoon sea breeze, expect it to be a touch **shifty and patchy** — especially in the lee just east and southeast of **Poppasquash Neck**, where the west shore blocks the wind and you'll find soft holes. For steadier air, drift a bit further out toward **Hog Island** and the harbor mouth. Your **best window is late morning through the early afternoon**; it may soften further as the day winds down.

## Tide & current
High water is at **08:29**, then a low at **14:14** — and that low is basically dead low (right around zero), so give the shallow edges near the harbor shorelines and any thin spots off the points a wide berth in the early-to-mid afternoon. Current is gentle inside the harbor and picks up out at the mouth: it's **ebbing (running seaward, toward the SSW)** through midday, going slack around **13:56** at Hog Island before turning to flood. Out in the **East Passage** the flow is noticeably stronger (over a knot on the ebb before it slacks around 15:00), so if you venture out past the island tips, factor that into your timing. With the light, mostly-northwest wind there's no meaningful wind-against-tide chop to worry about — it should stay comfortable and flat.

## Good to know
It's a beautiful early-fall day: **mostly sunny, highs 70–75°F, and no rain** in the forecast. That said, light wind plus strong sun means it can feel warm out there, so bring water, a hat, and sunscreen. Conditions are mild enough that this is a genuinely easy, forgiving day — but keep life jackets handy as always, especially with any kids aboard. **Head out late morning** to catch the best of the breeze, enjoy a leisurely afternoon, and aim to be back before it goes soft toward evening. Fair winds!
