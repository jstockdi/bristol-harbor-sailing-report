## Tactical call

**Course-agnostic:** Two levers point the same way tonight — **pressure lives S/seaward** (East Passage, off Poppasquash) and **fair flood favours the N/up-bay boat**. When the course lets you choose, **work toward the seaward breeze** for the extra 1–2 kt and cleaner air, and **avoid parking in the soft harbor-mouth/inner pocket** or the lee to the E/SE of Poppasquash Neck. On any seaward leg, the flood sets **NNE up-bay** — heading **N you get a lift/push; heading S you're pinched into it**, so lay the up-bay marks with the current under you and stay off the mid-Passage channel axis when clawing seaward. Keep your head out for the **veer toward the SSW** and shift with it rather than committing early to one geographic edge.

**If the course is set:**
- **N–S windward/leeward:** the E–W pressure gradient is the cross-course lever — the western/seaward edge (Poppasquash, toward the Passage) holds more breeze; ride the NNE flood on the up-bay (N) legs.
- **J-hook out of the harbor:** start in the near-slack inner harbor, then the hook into the mouth/Passage puts you in the stronger flood — expect the favoured edge to flip on the turn, and use the fair up-bay set once you round seaward and head back N.
- **Around Hog Island:** flood in every quadrant tonight (weak at the mouth, ~0.75 kt in the Passage) — call each leg by compass; the N/up-bay legs get the push, the S/seaward legs fight it, so protect the inside on the down-current turns.
- **Out-and-back (light air):** pressure dominates — get S to the East Passage breeze first; the light flood is a secondary, mostly-helpful factor on the return N.
