## The day
This is a mellow, family-friendly daysail — warm, sunny, and light. Speeds stay modest — averages around **5–6 kt** with occasional puffs into the low teens — so think easy full-sail sailing, drifter conditions in the lulls.

You'll find **a touch more breeze out toward Hog Island and the East Passage** (a knot or so stronger than up inside the harbor), so if things go slack in the harbor, nudging south toward the mouth usually finds a bit more push. Expect the usual **soft patch tucked in behind Poppasquash Neck (its E/SE side)** while the wind's northerly this morning — nothing wrong, just calm — so don't be surprised if you sit becalmed under that shore.

It smooths out as the current goes slack and turns.
