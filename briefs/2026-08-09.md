## The day
This is a proper feel-good daysail. Warm, sunny, and a mellow-to-just-spirited westerly-southwesterly breeze in the 8-knot range means a relaxed afternoon for a family cruise, with enough occasional puff (mid-teens) to keep things fun if you want to lean on it. Nothing threatening in the forecast — light rain chance, hot sun, and a breeze that's honest and steady. Pack water, sunscreen, and go enjoy it.

## Wind
Expect a gentle WSW breeze most of the day, out of roughly the west-southwest (around 260°) and holding a steady 8 knots or so, with gusts poking up to 15–16. It's light early — the live gauges at Newport and Conimicut were only reading 3–5 knots around dawn — but it's filling and actually running a touch ahead of the models this morning, so don't be surprised if it comes in a little friskier than 8 as the day warms. The **best window is late morning right through the afternoon**, when the breeze is most reliable.

Look for a **soft patch tucked in behind Poppasquash Neck** — with the wind out of the west, the western shore throws a wind shadow to its east/southeast side, so you'll find lighter, flukier air close under that shore and firmer, steadier breeze out toward the middle of the harbor and down by Hog Island. If you want the most consistent sailing, stay off the Poppasquash shore.

## Tide & current
Low water is at **10:30 and it's a very low one (about 0.2 ft)** — so early in your sail, give the shallow edges of the harbor and the flats near the shorelines a wide berth; it's a good morning to stay in known-deep water. High tide follows at **17:42** (a healthy 4.2 ft), so depth is on your side later in the afternoon.

Current in the harbor itself is gentle — slack near the mouth around 10:18, then a mild flood (setting NNE, up into the harbor) building through the afternoon and peaking around mid-afternoon. Out toward the East Passage the flow is a bit stronger, going slack around 11:30 before the flood fills in. With the breeze from the west and only a light current, there's no meaningful wind-against-tide chop to worry about — just easy water.

## Good to know
Gorgeous summer conditions: sunny to mostly sunny all day, highs in the mid-80s (81–87°F), and essentially no rain (3% at most). That's real heat, so bring plenty of water, a hat, and sunscreen, and keep an eye on anyone prone to overheating. With gusts touching the mid-teens it's worth having life jackets on the younger crew and easy to reach for everyone. A nice plan: **head out mid-to-late morning once the breeze fills**, enjoy the steady afternoon, and aim to be back by late afternoon around the evening high tide — relaxed, sunny, and no surprises.
