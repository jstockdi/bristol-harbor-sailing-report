Dress warm and waterproof, bring a way to check for fog banks rolling in, and keep VHF and a horn handy if it gets murky. Best plan: **head out late morning to early afternoon** while the breeze is steady and the fog is still patchy, keep the sail on the shorter side, and aim to be **back by mid-afternoon** — both to beat the worst of the rain risk and to stay off the shallow edges around that 13:50 low. A fun, brisk sail for the prepared crew.
