## The day
This is a gentle, easygoing day on the water — a mellow family daysail rather than a spirited one. The breeze is soft, sitting in the 6–7 knot range with occasional puffs to the low teens, so you'll be sailing full mains and enjoying the scenery more than hiking out. The models are in tight agreement (barely any spread) and the live buoys already back it up, so you can trust the light picture. The only asterisk is an afternoon shower-and-thunderstorm chance, so plan to be a bit flexible with your timing.

## Wind
Early on it's genuinely light — Newport's anemometer is reading barely 2 knots at mid-morning, with Conimicut a touch better around 3. Expect it to fill through late morning into a soft southerly to SW sea breeze in the 6–8 knot band, sliding around from SW toward more due-S as the afternoon goes on. Peak puffs reach the low-to-mid teens, so there's enough to have fun with, but this is nice-cruising air, not powered-up sailing. The **best window is roughly midday into early afternoon** (about noon to 3), when the breeze is most established. Expect a **soft patch tucked in behind Poppasquash Neck** to the west — if you drift into a hole under that shore, nudge out toward the middle of the harbor or down toward Hog Island, where the breeze off the East Passage is a touch steadier. There's marginally more air south toward Hog Island and the Passage mouth, so that's the place to point if things go glassy inside.

## Tide & current
High water is right in the middle of your day at **1:05 PM (about 4.0 ft)**, with lows bracketing it at **5:39 AM** and **6:47 PM (0.7 ft)**. Plenty of water for a relaxed sail, but as the afternoon low approaches keep an eye on the **shallow edges near the harbor fringes and around the Hog Island shoals** — give the shoreline a comfortable margin, especially later. Current is light and friendly in the harbor itself: near-slack inside, with a gentle flood (setting NNE, up into the harbor) easing off around **2 PM** at the mouth, then a mild ebb (SSW, seaward) building late afternoon. It picks up more noticeably out in the East Passage channel, but nothing dramatic for a casual sail. With such light wind you won't see meaningful wind-against-tide chop today — the water should stay pleasantly smooth.

## Good to know
Comfortable early-fall temps, around 72–77°F, so a light layer for the morning and you'll warm up nicely. Watch the sky: there's **patchy morning fog** and a **chance of showers and thunderstorms (up to ~44%)** building through the afternoon. Thunderstorms can bring sudden gusts and a quick wind-shift, so keep half an eye on the horizon and any dark, building cloud — if you see a cell developing, head in early and don't wait it out on the water. A good plan is to **launch late morning as the fog clears and the breeze fills, aim to be back by mid-to-late afternoon** ahead of the storm risk. Light air overall, but if a cell rolls through it can gust hard fast — so keep life jackets on and handy just in case.
