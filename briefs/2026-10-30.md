The gusts are the thing to respect — the models and NWS agree on **W 10–15 with puffs to 20**, and some fixes flash into the low-to-mid 20s, so it'll feel powered-up in the stronger pulses even though the baseline is comfortable. The breeze **veers through the day**, starting more SW and clocking around to NW by late afternoon — a classic post-frontal swing.

## Tide & current
**High water is at 11:38** (about 4.3 ft) and it falls all afternoon to a **low around 17:33 (just 0.3 ft)** — so keep an eye on **shallow spots near the harbor edges and shoals late in the day**, giving the shallows a wide berth as the water drops. At the **harbor mouth** the current goes **slack around 12:23**, then builds to a gentle **ebb (down-bay, toward the SW)** into the afternoon — never more than about a third of a knot, so nothing dramatic there. Out in the **East Passage** the ebb has more teeth (over a knot by mid-afternoon), setting SSW down the channel. With a W–NW wind the breeze and current are more across each other than opposed, so you shouldn't see nasty wind-against-tide chop — expect a modest 2-foot wind-wave at most.
