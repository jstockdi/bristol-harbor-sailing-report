## The day
It's a lovely day to get out, just a gentle one. Think mellow family daysail rather than a spirited rail-down romp: wall-to-wall sunshine, no rain in sight, and a soft westerly breeze that'll move you along without any drama. The one catch is the calendar — it's late November, so the air is cool and the daylight is short, so plan around an early finish. Grab it as a relaxed cruise and you'll have a great afternoon.

## Wind
Light and friendly all day. The forecast sits around **5 knots** with the odd puff poking into the low teens — so mostly drifter-to-easy-sailing, with occasional little gusts that'll perk you up without overpowering anything. Direction is **out of the W/WNW**, swinging gently through the day. Right now the real anemometers tell a nice story: Newport (down by the mouth) is reading about **7 knots WNW**, while Conimicut up the bay to the N is a touch breezier at **10–12 knots** with peaks near 16 — so you'll generally find a bit more pressure the further up-bay and out you get, and the lightest air tucked inside. Your best window is **midday into early afternoon**, when the sun has the breeze at its steadiest. Expect a **soft spot in the lee of Poppasquash Neck** — with the wind out of the W, the air goes light and patchy just to its E/SE, so don't be surprised to drift there and fill back in as you clear the shadow.

## Tide & current
Low water lands at **9:47 AM at just 0.4 ft** — very skinny — so if you're launching early, give the shallow harbor edges and any sandbars a wide berth until the water comes back up. From there the tide **floods and rises all afternoon to a high around 3.0 ft at 3:47 PM**, so depth only gets friendlier as the day goes on. Current is light inside the harbor — a weak flood nudging **up-bay (toward the N)** through the afternoon, going slack near the harbor mouth around **5:20 PM**. It runs a bit stronger out in the East Passage (toward Hope Island), but with such soft wind you won't see any real wind-against-tide chop — expect a smooth, calm surface all day.

## Good to know
Classic crisp autumn conditions: **sunny and clear**, highs around **62°F** dropping toward the low 50s, and **zero chance of rain**. The sun's out but November sun doesn't carry much warmth on the water, so **layer up** — a windproof shell and warm hands/feet make all the difference, and the water's cold now so dress for the splash, not the air. The breeze is gentle enough that life jackets are just good habit rather than a gust-driven necessity. Biggest thing to watch is **daylight**: the sun sets around 4:30 PM, so **head out late morning or early afternoon and aim to be tied up by 4:00 PM** while it's still bright and the breeze is at its best. Enjoy it!
