## The day
This is a crisp late-fall day with a steady northerly — a fun, spirited sail if you dress for it, but not a lazy family drift. There's real breeze (10–15 kt with gusts into the low-20s), and with air temps only in the low-40s it'll feel properly wintry on the water. If you like a bit of a blast and don't mind layering up, it's a good one; if you were hoping for a mellow ghost-around, this isn't that day. Keep it to experienced hands and stay nimble with the gusts.

## Wind
Northerly all day — it's blowing down off the Bristol and Poppasquash land to the N, so expect it **shifty and puffy** rather than smooth: lulls followed by firm gusts, and the direction wandering around N to NNW. Out in the open water the breeze is steadier and stronger — the harbor mouth off **Hog Island Shoal** and down into the **East Passage** are running a few knots more (and the live anemometers up the bay are already seeing mid-teens with gusts near 20). Inside the harbor itself it's a touch softer and gustier. The classic soft patch today sits **just downwind (E/SE) of the Poppasquash shore**, which blankets the breeze — expect to park a bit if you tuck in there, then get a shove as you clear its shadow. Best window is **late morning into the early afternoon** while the breeze is most consistent; it eases toward evening. Honestly a day to think about a reef or a smaller headsail if your boat powers up easily.

## Tide & current
High water is right at **10:02 AM** and low is **3:42 PM**, so the water's dropping all through the prime sailing hours — mind the **shallow edges near the harbor fringes and any thin spots** as the afternoon low approaches. Current runs hardest through the **entrance gut by Hog Island** and down the **East Passage channel**; it's near-slack inside the harbor. The tide turns to ebb (seaward, SSW) around **midday** and runs out through the early afternoon — that ebb flows roughly *with* the northerly, so it won't kick up much chop. Earlier in the morning, though, the up-bay flood runs *against* the N wind through the mouth, so expect a short, steeper little chop out toward Hog Island before the turn around 11-ish. NWS is calling seas around 3 ft out in the bay, so it'll be lumpier the farther south you venture.

## Good to know
Dress for cold: low-40s air, a brisk wind, and plenty of spray potential means **full winter layers, gloves, and a warm hat** — the wind chill will be the real story. Skies are mostly cloudy with a small chance of light rain (under ~30%), so no thunderstorm worries, but bring a waterproof top. With gusts to the low-20s, this is a **life-jacket-on, stay-clipped-in kind of day** — keep crew alert and hands ready to ease. Shortening days this time of year, too, so plan to **head out mid-morning and be back well before the light goes**. Have fun, keep it warm, and enjoy that bright blustery bay air.
