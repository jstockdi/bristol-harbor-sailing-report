## The day
This is a sailable day with a caveat: there's a nice, steady breeze in the 8–9 knot range with gusts pushing the high teens, so it'll feel like a spirited-but-friendly daysail rather than a drifter. The catch is the sky — patchy fog early, mostly cloudy, and a real chance of rain moving through, so treat it as a "grab your window and keep an eye up" kind of day. Cool, gray, and breezy: good sailing if you dress for it and don't mind cutting it short if a shower rolls in.

Give the shallow edges near the harbor shores and any skinny spots off the points a wide berth as you head back in. With the ebb running SSW against an ESE breeze, you may notice a little **short chop building out toward the mouth and Passage** in the afternoon; nothing alarming, just don't be surprised by the bumpier ride.

## Good to know
It's a cool one — air only in the upper 60s (66–69°F) — under mostly cloudy skies with patchy morning fog, so bring layers and a **waterproof jacket**; rain is very much on the table today (high chance through the day). Keep an eye on visibility if the fog lingers early. With gusts into the high teens, it's a sensible day to **keep life jackets on**, especially with kids or newer crew aboard. Plan to **head out mid-to-late morning** to catch the best of the breeze and beat the wettest weather, and aim to **be back by early-to-mid afternoon** — that keeps you ahead of both the falling tide over the shallows and any rain moving in.
