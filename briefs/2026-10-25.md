## The day
This is a mellow one — a gentle, bluebird October day with light air and flat water. Winds hover around 4 knots most of the day with occasional little puffs into the low teens, so it's a proper drifter rather than a spirited sail. If you're after a peaceful cruise under sail, soaking up the sun with a coffee in hand, this is a treat. If you want to really move, you may spend some of it ghosting along — bring patience (and maybe a paddle for a dinghy). Skies are sunny and clear with no rain in the picture.

## Wind
Light all day, truthfully. The morning starts out of the N/NW and the breeze slowly backs around through the day, swinging toward the S by late afternoon — a soft, light southerly rather than the big summer sea breeze. Average speeds sit near 4 knots across the harbor and out toward Hog Island and the East Passage, with brief gusts to 12–13 knots keeping you honest. It's a drifter-to-barely-nice day; you'll want your lightest sails and a light touch on the helm. The best of it is likely **late morning into early afternoon**, before things go light and shifty as the direction transitions. Expect the usual soft patch tucked under the **Poppasquash shore to the west** when the wind has any north or west in it — stay out toward the middle of the harbor or south toward the harbor mouth where there's a touch more pressure. The live anemometers at Newport and up at Conimicut both read very light this morning (under 5 knots), right in line with the forecast, so confidence in "it'll stay gentle" is high.

## Tide & current
High water was early at 07:27, with a **low at 13:27 that's quite low (about -0.1 ft)** — so through the early afternoon give the shallow harbor edges and any skinny spots near Poppasquash a wide berth; stay to deeper water. A second high rolls in at 19:51. Current is modest at the harbor mouth (under half a knot), ebbing seaward through late morning, slack around 12:49, then filling back into the harbor later. Out in the **East Passage** the current runs stronger — an ebb to about 1.2 knots setting SSW/seaward until it goes slack near 14:00. With such light wind there's nothing to worry about in the way of chop, but if you venture out toward the Passage before early afternoon, note the ebb will gently carry you seaward — easy to drift further than you planned in light air, so keep an eye on your position.

## Good to know
A crisp, pretty fall day: sunny and clear, with air temps from the high 40s up to around 59°F. It'll feel cool on the water once you're moving, so dress in layers and bring a windproof top — the water's getting colder this time of year, so think warm. No rain and no thunderstorm risk, and waves a foot or less all day, so it's as benign as it gets. A good plan: **head out mid-morning to catch the best of the light breeze through early afternoon, and aim to be back by late afternoon** as the wind goes soft and the sun sits lower (sunset comes early now). It won't be gusty enough to worry about, but with cold water this late in the season, wear your life jacket and let someone ashore know your plan. Enjoy the quiet — it'll be a calm, scenic one.
