## The day
This is a really nice one to get out on. A dependable southwesterly is already filling, the models all agree (barely a knot of spread), and the live anemometers down at Newport and up at Conimicut are reading a healthy 9–14 kt — so you're looking at a classic, well-powered fall daysail under mostly sunny skies. Not a drifter, not a white-knuckle day; just a spirited, enjoyable breeze that'll keep a cruising boat moving nicely. Great conditions for anyone from a relaxed family sail (tuck in a reef if you like it mellow) to folks who enjoy a bit of heel.

## Wind
Expect a steady **SW/SSW** breeze through the day, building into the afternoon. Average is around **8–9 kt** across the harbor and out toward the East Passage, with gusts into the **mid-to-high teens** (NWS has it SW 10–15 with gusts to 20). In plain terms: comfortably **powered-up** most of the day, with the odd gust that'll have you easing the sheet. The breeze is a touch stronger and more consistent out toward Hog Island and the East Passage, and softer tucked up inside. The **best window is roughly noon through late afternoon**, when the sea breeze is most settled. Watch for a **soft patch in the lee of Poppasquash Neck** (the west shore) — if you sail into its shadow you'll lose a few knots, so give that shoreline a little room and the breeze will fill back in as you clear it.

## Tide & current
High water is early (05:41) and again at **18:10**, with a notably **low** low tide at **11:52 (just 0.3 ft)** — so midday is the time to **stay well off the shallow harbor edges and shoals** near Hog Island; give any skinny water a wide berth around lunchtime. Current is light inside the harbor but runs harder through the mouth and the East Passage: it's **ebbing (setting SSW, seaward)** through the morning until slack around **11:10 at the harbor mouth / 12:22 in the Passage**, then **floods back up-bay (NNE)** into the afternoon. In the morning the SW wind blows against that ebbing current, so expect a bit of **short, steep chop near the harbor mouth and out in the Passage** — nothing alarming, just a heads-up. Things smooth out once the flood takes over in the afternoon.

## Good to know
A crisp, pleasant fall day: highs around **67°F**, lows near 60, mostly **sunny to partly sunny** with only a slight (~20%) chance of a passing shower — no thunderstorm concerns. Dress in layers; it'll feel cooler out on the water with that breeze, especially later. With gusts into the high teens, it's a **good day to have life jackets on**, particularly for kids and newer crew. A nice plan: **head out around midday** as the breeze settles and the chop eases past slack, and aim to be **back by late afternoon** — you'll have the flood helping you home and plenty of daylight, with high water again around 6 PM making the docks easy.
