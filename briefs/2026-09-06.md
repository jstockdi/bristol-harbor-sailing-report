## The day
This is a mellow, easy-going daysail — think light-air pottering rather than a spirited rail-down romp. A soft northerly of around 5–6 kt sits over the whole harbor, so boats will glide rather than heel, and the sky is friendly (mostly sunny, cool, next to no rain). Perfect for a relaxed sail, a picnic aboard, or teaching someone the ropes. If you're hoping to get powered up, temper expectations: it'll be a drifter's day.

## Wind
Expect a light **N/NW breeze** most of the day — roughly 5–6 kt on average with occasional puffs to 10–11 kt, so nothing that'll overpower you. The direction gradually **backs from the N toward the W/NW** as the afternoon goes on. The live anemometers this morning back that up: Newport is reading a light NNE 6–7 kt and Conimicut up-bay a NNW 8 kt, matching the forecast nicely — good confidence in a light day.

There's a touch more air out toward the **harbor mouth and the East Passage (to the S/SSW)** than tucked up inside, so if you want the best of the breeze, drift out toward Hog Island. The classic soft spot to watch: with the wind out of the N/NW, the **east side of Poppasquash Neck (the west shore)** sits in the land's wind shadow — expect a lull and patchy air close under that shore, and a little more life as you slide back out into open water. Best window to be out is roughly **midday into mid-afternoon**, when the sun's had time to give the breeze whatever help it can.

## Tide & current
Low water is early, around **09:15** and it's a genuinely low one (about half a foot), so give the shallow edges near the harbor fringes and any thin spots a wide berth if you're heading out early — you'll have more water under you as the tide fills toward the afternoon **high around 16:22**.

Current in the harbor itself is gentle. Out at the mouth by Hog Island the flow goes near-slack around **09:00**, then a light flood (setting NNE, up into the harbor) builds through the afternoon. Down in the **East Passage** the current runs a bit firmer — slack around **10:00**, then flood through midday. Nothing dramatic, and with such light wind you won't see any nasty wind-against-tide chop — just be aware the Passage nudges you around more than the calm inner harbor does.

## Good to know
A crisp, comfortable early-fall day: highs around **69°F**, dipping toward the low 60s, with **mostly sunny** skies and only a **6% chance of rain** — no thunderstorm worries. Bring a layer, as it'll feel cooler out on the water in that northerly, plus sunscreen and water for the bright sun. Nothing here is gusty enough to be a handful, but life jackets on for the kids and newer sailors is always the easy call. Head out mid-morning once the tide's building for more water beneath you, enjoy the middle of the day, and aim to be back at the dock before the light softens in the evening.
