## The day
This is a lovely, low-key day to be out — mostly sunny, comfortable, and a soft easterly breeze that never really gets up to a handful. Think relaxed family daysail rather than a spirited rail-down ripper: light enough for anyone aboard to be at ease, with just enough puff mid-day to keep you moving nicely. If you like your sailing calm and scenic, this is your kind of afternoon.

## Wind
Expect a light **east to east-northeast** breeze, roughly **6–8 knots** most of the day with occasional puffs poking up toward **12 knots** — a genuine "nice, easy" day, drifting to gently powered-up in the gusts, never anything to reef for. The morning obs back this up nicely: Newport is already reading a steady 7–8 kt ENE and Conimicut about the same, so forecast and reality are shaking hands (models agree closely too). The breeze slowly **veers through the day, edging from ENE toward the E/ESE** by late afternoon, and it tends to soften a touch after 5 PM.

Because it's an easterly rather than the usual afternoon southwesterly, the calmest patches will be tucked close under the **Bristol (eastern) waterfront**, where the land takes the edge off — nice for a mellow drift, less so if you want to keep way on. For steadier air, stay out in more open water toward **Hog Island** and the harbor mouth.

## Tide & current
High water is right around midday (**12:09 PM, ~4.0 ft**), sliding down to a **low near 5:43 PM at just 0.5 ft**. That late-afternoon low is worth a note: give the shallow shore edges and any skinny spots near the harbor fringes a wide berth as the water drops toward evening.

Current in the harbor mouth is gentle — a light flood (up-bay) easing to **slack around 1:12 PM**, then a mild ebb (seaward) building through the afternoon. Out in the **East Passage** the flow is noticeably stronger, with the ebb running down toward **1 knot-plus** by mid-afternoon (peak around 2:51 PM). With today's light easterly there's no real wind-against-tide chop to worry about, so it'll stay comfortable — just know that if you wander out into the Passage, the current there has more say than the breeze does.

## Good to know
Warm but not hot — highs around **73°F**, mostly to partly sunny skies, and **no rain in the forecast**. Great day for sunscreen, hats, and water aboard. Nothing gusty enough to demand it, but life jackets are always the easy call, especially with kids or newer sailors along.

A relaxed plan: **head out mid-to-late morning** to catch the best of the breeze through the sunny middle of the day, and aim to be **back before that late-afternoon low water** (before ~5 PM) so the dropping tide doesn't catch you near the shallows. Enjoy it — this is about as friendly as Bristol Harbor gets.
