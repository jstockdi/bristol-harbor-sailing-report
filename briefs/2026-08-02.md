## The day
This is a really nice one — a proper family daysail with enough breeze to keep the boat moving and heeling gently without ever feeling like a handful. A steady southerly of around 9–10 knots holds all day, the models are in near-total agreement (spread under a knot), so you can plan with confidence. Warm, a touch grey, maybe a passing shower — but nothing that should keep you off the water. Go enjoy it.

## Wind
Expect a soft, warm southerly (roughly S to SSW) building through the morning and settling into a friendly 9–10 kt for most of the day, with gusts poking into the mid-teens. That's a lovely "nice sailing" band — powered up enough to be fun, mellow enough for anyone aboard to relax. The live anemometers this morning show the classic Bristol pattern: the up-bay reading at Conimicut is already a solid ~12 kt while Newport at the Passage mouth is a gentler ~6 kt, so the breeze fills and firms up as the day goes on. Out toward Hog Island and the East Passage you'll find the most consistent pressure; up inside the harbor it's a touch lighter and shiftier. The one predictable soft spot: tuck in close under the **Poppasquash shore (the west side)** and you'll sail into its wind shadow — expect a lull there before the breeze clocks back in as you slide out from behind the point. Best window to be out is late morning right through mid-afternoon.

## Tide & current
High water is at **10:59 am** and it drops to a proper low of just **0.3 ft at 4:31 pm** — so late afternoon is skinny-water time. Give the harbor edges, flats, and any shallow marks a wide berth as the tide bottoms out. Current is mild inside the harbor itself but picks up through the **entrance gut by Hog Island Shoal** and noticeably more in the **East Passage**. The tide turns to ebb (setting SSW, seaward) around **2:30 pm** near the mouth — and since today's breeze is a southerly, that afternoon ebb runs into the wind. Out in the Passage that wind-against-tide combo can kick up a short, bouncy chop, so if you've got littler crew aboard, the flatter water is up inside the harbor and around Hog Island before mid-afternoon.

## Good to know
Warm and summery — mid-to-upper 70s, mostly cloudy skies, with just a **20% chance of a stray shower or thunderstorm**. Keep half an eye on the western sky; if you see a cell building, head in early, as summer pop-ups can bring a quick gust. Otherwise it's an easy, comfortable day: sun's soft behind the clouds but bring water and a hat anyway. With gusts into the mid-teens it's worth having everyone's **life jackets on and handy** — nothing alarming, just good practice on a breezy afternoon. A great plan: head out mid-to-late morning to catch the building breeze and flatter water, and aim to be back around the low-tide/ebb turn in mid-afternoon before the Passage gets lumpy. Have fun out there.
