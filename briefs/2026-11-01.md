## The day

Expect a soft, drifter-to-light day: lovely if you want a peaceful ghost around the harbor with the sails up and nowhere to be, a little frustrating if you're itching for a rail-down romp. Bring patience and enjoy the sunshine.

Light **west** breeze all day, roughly 5–10 kt on paper but likely nearer the **3–6 kt** end given what the live gauges are showing. It stays light right through to evening, so no reefing worries today.

With the breeze so light and out of the west, there's no meaningful wind-against-tide chop to catch you out — it'll stay comfortable and flat, around a foot of ripple.
