## The day
This is a solid autumn daysail — not a drifter and not a handful, just a steady, usable breeze with a cool October feel. There's a gentle SE wind filling the bay, enough to keep you moving happily without being a workout. The catch is the sky: it's mostly cloudy with a real chance of showers, and the breeze firms up into the evening, so this is a day to enjoy the midday window and plan to be back on the mooring before the weather turns.

## Wind
Expect a friendly SE breeze averaging around 7 kt with gusts into the low-to-mid teens — comfortable "nice sailing" conditions for most boats, with the odd puff that keeps you honest out toward the harbor mouth and Hog Island (gusts there run a touch higher, up to ~15 kt). The live anemometers are already reading a bit stronger than the models suggested — Newport at the Passage mouth is seeing 8–9 kt and Conimicut up-bay 10–12 kt with gusts to 14 — so lean toward the stronger end and don't be surprised by powered-up moments down near the East Passage. Through the day the wind slowly backs from the SE toward more of an E. The softest, fluttery air will be tucked right under the downwind shore — close in along the Bristol waterfront with this SE/E flow — so if you go looking for a mellow drift that's where to find it; for cleaner, steadier pressure stay out in the open harbor and toward Hog Island. **Best window: late morning through mid-afternoon (roughly 10:30–15:00).** After that the breeze builds (NWS calls E 15–20 kt by evening), so it's not the time to be caught out late.

## Tide & current
High water was at 08:48 (about 4.5 ft) and it drains to a very low tide at 14:33 — only about a tenth of a foot, so this is a big low. Give the shallow edges of the harbor and any skinny spots near shore a wide berth through the early afternoon; stay in the marked, deeper water. Current is gentle inside the harbor around the late-morning slack (~10:00), then an ebb sets in and runs seaward (SSW, down-bay) through the early afternoon — it's modest at the harbor mouth but noticeably stronger out in the East Passage (slack there around 15:30). With the SE/E breeze pushing against that outgoing ebb, expect some short, lumpy chop out in the Passage near Hog Island during the late morning and midday — nothing alarming, just a bit of bounce so you're not caught off guard. It settles as the current eases toward mid-afternoon.

## Good to know
It's a cool, grey fall day — highs only around 60–62°F — so dress warm and bring layers and a waterproof shell; it'll feel chilly out on the water under cloud. Skies are mostly cloudy with a decent chance of rain showers (up to ~70%), and the NWS mentions patchy fog and heavier showers moving in tonight, so keep an eye to windward and don't push the afternoon. With gusts into the teens and building breeze later, it's a good day to have life jackets on, especially for kids and newer crew. Head out late morning, enjoy the steady midday breeze, and aim to be back and secured by mid-to-late afternoon before the wind firms up and the weather closes in.
