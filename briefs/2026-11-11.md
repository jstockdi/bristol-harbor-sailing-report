## The day
This is a calm, low-key autumn daysail rather than a thrilling one. A gentle W/NW breeze sits in the 5–7 kt range most of the day with occasional puffs into the low teens, so you'll have enough to move a boat without ever feeling pressed. The catch is the season, not the wind: air in the low-to-mid 50s and a sun that sets early means this is a "dress warm, keep it short, enjoy the quiet harbor" kind of outing. Perfect for a relaxed family sail or a peaceful solo drift.

## Wind
Expect light-to-moderate W/NW breeze through the day — roughly a gentle **5–7 kt average** with the odd puff to 12–14 kt, so mostly a pleasant soft-sailing pace with brief moments of life. Direction starts out of the **NW (~300°)** and slowly backs toward the **WSW (~255°)** by late afternoon. The live anemometers are reading on the lighter side right now (Newport around 3 kt, Conimicut around 4–5 kt), and the models broadly agree it's a light day, so don't expect it to suddenly pipe up. Your best window is **late morning through early afternoon**, when there's the most to work with before the breeze fades into the evening. On a W/NW wind, look for a **soft, sheltered patch tucked under the Poppasquash Neck shore (to its E/SE)** — nice if you want flat water, but you'll find steadier air out toward the harbor mouth and Hog Island.

## Tide & current
High water was at **08:43** (3.9 ft) and it draws down to a **low around 14:25** (just 0.3 ft), so watch your depth near the shallow harbor edges and any skinny spots around the shoals through the early afternoon — give the margins a wide berth near low tide. Current at the harbor mouth is **slack around 10:00**, then gently ebbs **SSW (seaward)** toward midday before slacking again near **14:00**. Out in the East Passage the ebb is a bit firmer (up to ~1 kt, slack ~15:20). With the breeze light and out of the W/NW and the ebb setting SSW, there's no real wind-against-tide chop to worry about — just a little set to be aware of if you drift out toward the mouth.

## Good to know
Cool and seasonal: highs only around **52–57°F**, so layer up with warm, windproof gear and gloves — on the water it'll feel colder than on the dock. Skies range from mostly sunny to cloudy with only a small (~22%) chance of a passing shower, so no thunderstorm worries today. The wind stays friendly, but if you catch one of those low-teens puffs you'll appreciate having **life jackets on** anyway — always smart in cold-water season. Aim to **head out late morning** for the best breeze and warmest part of the day, and **be back by mid-afternoon**: November light goes early, and you don't want to be sorting lines in the cold and dark.
