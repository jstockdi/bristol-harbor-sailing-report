## The day
This is a lovely, low-key day to get out — sunshine, warm air in the low 80s, and virtually no rain risk. The breeze is on the light side, so think relaxed drifter-to-daysailer rather than a spirited rail-down romp. If you like calm water, easy handling, and a picnic-on-the-water pace, you'll be happy. If you're itching for power, you may find yourself wishing for a few more knots — but for a gentle afternoon float it's just about perfect.

## Wind
Expect a soft southwesterly filling in through the day, averaging around 5–6 knots with the odd puff into the low teens — enough to keep you moving without ever feeling overpowered. This morning the real anemometers are reading even lighter (Newport around 3 kt, Conimicut ~4–5 kt) out of the NW, and the models look to have been slightly over-blowing overnight, so treat the early hours as genuinely light and drifty. The sea breeze should settle into its usual SW pattern by early afternoon, and there's a touch more consistency out toward Hog Island and the East Passage than up in the harbor. Best window to be out is roughly **noon through late afternoon**, when the breeze is steadiest. Watch for a soft, glassy patch tucked in under the **Poppasquash shore to the west** — if you park there, ghost your way back out toward the middle of the harbor or down toward the mouth to find the puffs again.

## Tide & current
Low water is at **11:36 this morning and it's a big one — essentially dead low (0.0 ft)** — so give the shallow edges and any skinny spots near the harbor fringes a wide berth around midday. The tide then floods all afternoon to a **4.5 ft high at 18:43**, so depths only improve as the day goes on. Current is gentle and cooperative: it's near **slack at the harbor mouth around 11:26**, then a mild flood builds up-bay (into the harbor, NNE) through the afternoon. Nothing dramatic here — flow is stronger out in the East Passage channel than inside the harbor, but with such light wind you won't see much wind-against-tide chop. Easy going.

## Good to know
Sunny to partly cloudy, temps climbing from around 79°F to 85°F, and essentially no rain (1%). It'll be a warm one, so bring water, sunscreen, and a hat — the sun is the main thing to manage today, not the weather. With winds this light, conditions are very forgiving, but keep life jackets handy for the kids and anyone who wants them. A relaxed plan: **head out around late morning to midday** as the breeze fills, enjoy the steadiest air through the afternoon, and aim to be **back by early evening** with the rising tide giving you a comfortable margin at the docks. Enjoy it out there.
