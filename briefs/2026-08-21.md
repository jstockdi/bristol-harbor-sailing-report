## The day
It's a solid sailing day with one big caveat: the sky. The breeze is a steady, spirited northerly in the low-to-mid teens — plenty of push for a fun, powered-up daysail without ever being scary — but showers are likely (about a 6-in-10 chance) and the air is on the cool side at 69–72°F. If you're happy in foulies and don't mind a passing squall, you'll get a genuinely nice sail. If you want a dry, sun-on-your-face lazy afternoon, this isn't quite that day.

## Wind
Expect a **N to NNE breeze** all day, averaging around **10–11 kt** with **gusts pushing 21 kt** in the puffs — so plan for "nicely powered up, hike in the gusts" rather than a drifter. The models are in tight agreement and the live buoys back it up: Newport reads about 10 kt from the NNE this morning and Conimicut ~11 kt from the N, so this is a well-established northerly, not a fickle sea breeze. Because it's coming off the land to the north, it'll be a touch **shifty and puffy** — nothing dramatic, just stay alert to the gusts. The reliable **soft spot is tucked in behind Poppasquash Neck**, where the western shore throws a wind shadow to its E/SE; expect a light, patchy lull if you sail up under that shore, and steadier pressure the moment you clear back out into the open harbor or head down toward Hog Island and the East Passage, where it's a knot or two stronger. The breeze holds all day, so there's no rush on the wind — pick your window around the rain, not the air.

## Tide & current
Low water was early at **08:00 (about 0.9 ft)** and the tide fills all morning to a **high around 15:30 (3.2 ft)** before easing back toward the next low at 22:08 — so through your whole afternoon you'll have comfortable depth over the shallow harbor edges. Currents are gentle: the harbor mouth off Hog Island runs a lazy **flood (up-bay, into the harbor) from about 08:30 until it turns near 16:45**, then eases to ebb. Down in the **East Passage the flow is a bit livelier** (roughly 0.6–0.8 kt) but still modest. One friendly heads-up: with the wind out of the N and the midday current flooding to the NNE, you've got **wind-against-tide** through the afternoon, so expect a **short, choppy little slop** — most noticeable out in the Passage and around the island tips. Nothing a keelboat won't shrug off, but it'll feel bouncier than the flat inner harbor.

## Good to know
Cool and damp is the theme — **69–72°F, overcast, and rain showers likely**, so bring layers and real rain gear and keep phones/valuables in a dry bag. No thunderstorms are flagged, so this is nuisance rain rather than anything threatening, but showers can knock the visibility down, so keep an eye upwind. With gusts into the low 20s and choppy patches out toward the Passage, this is a **life-jackets-on kind of day**, especially for kids and anyone new aboard. Best plan: **head out mid-to-late morning**, enjoy the steady breeze, and use the radar on your phone to duck the heavier cells — **aim to be back on the mooring by mid-to-late afternoon** so you're not caught out if the rain settles in for the evening.
