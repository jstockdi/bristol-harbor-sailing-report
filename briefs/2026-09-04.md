## The day
This is a mellow, easygoing daysail — the kind of soft late-summer afternoon that's lovely for a relaxed cruise but won't have you hiking hard. Winds are light out of the northwest (roughly 5–7 knots with occasional puffs to low double digits), skies are sunny and warm, and the water will be calm. If you're after a spirited rail-down sail this isn't it, but for a peaceful poke around the harbor with friends or family, it's about as pleasant as it gets.

## Wind
Expect a light **northwesterly** most of the day — think gentle drifter-to-nice-sailing range, 5–7 knots, with puffs that briefly nudge toward 10–12. Because it's coming off the land to the northwest, it'll be a touch **shifty and puffy** rather than a steady sea breeze, so stay awake to the little wind lines on the water. The live picture this morning shows Newport reading a soft ~5 knots while Conimicut up the bay is gustier — a reminder the breeze is patchy and models may be over-promising a knot or two, so plan for the lighter end.

Best window to be out is **late morning through mid-afternoon**, when whatever breeze there is will be steadiest and the sun's warmest. The obvious soft spot: tuck out of the lee **east and southeast of the Poppasquash shore**, where that northwest wind gets blanketed by the land — you'll find more consistent air out in the open harbor and toward Hog Island to the south.

## Tide & current
Low water was early this morning, and the tide **fills all day to a comfortable high around 2 PM** (nearly 4 ft), so you'll have plenty of water under you through the whole window — no shallow worries near the harbor edges this afternoon. Inside the harbor the current is gentle and won't push you around much. If you wander out toward the **harbor mouth by Hog Island** or into the **East Passage**, note the tide turns from flood to ebb early-to-mid afternoon (slack around 2:30–3 PM out there), and the Passage runs a bit stronger — but with this light NW breeze there's no meaningful wind-against-tide chop to catch you out. Flat, friendly water.

## Good to know
Gorgeous conditions topside: highs around **80°F**, mostly sunny, and essentially **no rain** (1% chance). That much sun and warmth means **hat, sunglasses, water, and sunscreen** are your real safety gear today — it's easy to cook out there on a calm day. Winds are light enough that this is genuinely casual, but keep life jackets handy as always, especially with kids aboard. Head out **late morning** to catch the best of the breeze and be back before it softens toward evening — plan to be off the water by **5–5:30 PM** as the light air fades. Enjoy it.
