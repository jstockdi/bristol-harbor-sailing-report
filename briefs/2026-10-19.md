## The day
This is a lovely October day to get out — mostly sunny, dry, and a dependable northeasterly breeze in the 7–9 kt range with gusts poking into the mid-teens. Call it a spirited-but-manageable daysail: enough wind to make the boat go and put a grin on your face, without being a handful. The one thing to respect is that NE/N wind comes off the Bristol and Poppasquash land, so it'll be a bit shifty and puffy rather than smooth — fun if you like trimming, just stay awake for the gusts.

## Wind
Expect **NE to ENE wind all day, averaging around 8 kt** and gradually veering a touch toward the NE (roughly from NNE to NE) through the afternoon. Gusts run to about 15 kt, so you'll get stretches of easy cruising punctuated by livelier puffs — "nicely powered up" rather than reef-it territory. The live anemometers back this up: Newport is reading about 7 kt gusting 11 at the Passage mouth, Conimicut about the same up-bay, and the models agree closely, so confidence is good. The **best window is late morning through mid-afternoon**; note the NWS has the breeze building toward 15–20 kt later this evening and overnight, so plan to be in before it stiffens after dark. **Soft spot to expect:** tuck in close under the western Poppasquash shore and you'll find a lee — the breeze goes light and patchy there before filling back in as you work out into the harbor.

## Tide & current
Low water was early this morning (**8:04 am, ~1.0 ft**), then it rises all day to a **high around 2:53 pm (~2.9 ft)** before easing off toward the next low at 9:36 pm. That means plenty of water under you through the whole daysail — good news for the shallow edges of the harbor, though give the skinny margins near shore their usual respect as the tide drops again late afternoon. **Current is gentle today** (well under a knot at the harbor mouth, a bit more in the East Passage channel): it's flooding up-bay to the NNE through about 2 pm, then goes slack and turns to ebb seaward in the early evening. Because the NE wind blows roughly against that up-bay flood, you may notice a little **light wind-against-tide chop out toward Hog Island and the East Passage** during the early afternoon — nothing dramatic, just a short, bouncy ride rather than glassy, so it's no surprise if it's a touch lumpier out in the open than inside the harbor.

## Good to know
A proper crisp fall day: **mostly sunny, highs only around 60°F**, and essentially no rain (1% chance) or storms to worry about. Dress for the water, not the sun — layers, a warm hat, and something windproof will make the difference, as 55–61°F feels cooler out on a breezy boat. With gusts into the mid-teens and puffy wind coming off the land, this is a **life-jackets-on day**, especially for kids and anyone less steady. Head out late morning to catch the best of the breeze and the high water, and aim to be back at the dock by late afternoon before the wind builds into the evening. Enjoy it — these bright, breezy October sails are some of the best of the season.
