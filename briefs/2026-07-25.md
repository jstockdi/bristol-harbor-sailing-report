## The day
This is a proper feel-good day on the water — wall-to-wall sun, a comfortable low-to-mid-70s, and no weather to fret about. The breeze is on the gentle side, so think easy family daysail rather than a spirited hike-out romp: enough to fill the sails and ghost pleasantly around the harbor and out toward Hog Island, without ever getting your heart rate up. If you want a relaxing afternoon with kids, guests, or a picnic aboard, today delivers.

## Wind
It's genuinely light this morning — the real anemometers are reading only a couple of knots (Newport ~3 kt, Conimicut barely 1–2 kt), so if you head out at 10 you may be drifting for a bit. The good news is the classic Bristol sea breeze should build through late morning and into the afternoon, filling from the **S/SSW** up the East Passage and swinging around from the north through the morning to settle into that southerly by midday. Expect a comfortable **6–8 kt** once it's established, with occasional puffs to around 12 — nice, easy sailing that never needs a reef.

**Best window: roughly 12:30–17:00**, once the breeze has filled in. You'll usually find a touch more air out toward **Hog Island and south** than up inside the harbor, so if things feel sticky up top, drifting toward the mouth often finds you a knot or two more. The reliable soft spot: tucked in close under the **western Poppasquash shore**, which sits in a wind shadow — expect a lull there and don't be surprised if the breeze wanders as you clear it. The forecast models are in tight agreement today, so you can trust this light-but-pleasant picture.

## Tide & current
Low water lands at **10:42 (just 0.7 ft)** — quite low — so if you're heading out first thing, give the shallow harbor edges and any skinny spots near the shoreline a wide berth. From there the tide floods all afternoon up to a **3.4 ft high at 18:06**, so depth is on your side as the day goes on. Current is gentle in the harbor: it's a light ebb (seaward) through late morning, goes slack at the mouth around **11:05**, then floods gently up-bay through the afternoon. Nothing dramatic — the strongest flow lives out in the **East Passage** channel, where it runs a bit harder, but with today's soft breeze you won't see meaningful wind-against-tide chop. Smooth water all around.

## Good to know
Just about ideal comfort: **sunny and clear, 71–77°F, zero rain chance**, no thunderstorms in the picture. The main thing to pack for is **sun** — hats, sunscreen, water — because there's nothing to hide behind on a clear day like this. Head out late morning if you don't mind drifting for the breeze to build, or wait until around lunchtime for a livelier sail, and aim to be back before the light softens in the evening. Conditions stay gentle enough that this is an easy, low-stress day for less-experienced crew — kids in life jackets, everyone relaxed. Enjoy it.
